核心贡献

English: Actions encoded as RGB Gaussian heatmaps in multi-view image space; fine-tunes Wan 2.2 to co-generate observation and action videos; recovers control by multi-view ray casting.

核心代码实现

类型：论文伪代码（按 §3 结构化）。zero-shot 策略：视频骨干直接当策略。
