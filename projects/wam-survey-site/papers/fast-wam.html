Fast-WAM：世界动作模型需要测试时未来想象吗？

Fast-WAM: Do World Action Models Need Test-time Future Imagination?
arXiv:2603.16666 ｜ IIIS Tsinghua / Galaxea AI ｜ 2026-03 ｜ 代码对比页 ｜ arXiv · 项目页 · GitHub

核心贡献

中文：用受控变体把「训练期视频共训」与「推理期未来想象」解耦：去掉视频共训掉 8 点，去掉测试时想象只差约 1 点；无具身预训练达到 RoboTwin 91.8 / LIBERO 97.6，延迟 190ms。

English: Decouples training-time video co-training from inference-time imagination via controlled variants; recovers most of the benefit of pretrained WAM pipelines without embodied pretraining, at 190 ms latency.

原文断言（摘要）+ 中译

原文（Abstract，verbatim）we propose Fast-WAM, a WAM architecture that retains video co-training during training but skips future prediction at test time.
中译（译者译，非论文原文语言）我们提出 Fast-WAM——一种在训练期保留视频共训、但在测试时跳过未来预测的 WAM 架构。

核心机制（原文 + 中译）

原文（论文方法节，verbatim）At inference time, Fast-WAM removes the future video branch entirely: only the clean latent tokens of the first observation frame are retained and passed through the video backbone once to produce latent world features for the action expert.
中译（译者译）推理时整体移除未来视频分支：只保留第一帧的干净潜 token，过一次视频骨干产生潜世界特征，供动作专家使用。
