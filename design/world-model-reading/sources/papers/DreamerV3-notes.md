# DreamerV3 精读笔记（arXiv 2301.04104）

> 唯一数据源：`auto_docs/papers/txt/DreamerV3.txt`。所有英文引文逐字复制自该 txt，行号以该 txt 为准；论文未写的内容一律标「原文未写」；笔者的理解标【推断】。

## 0. 一页总结（problem → method → experiment → takeaway）

**问题**：RL 算法迁移到新域需大量调参，是应用瓶颈；论文目标是「固定超参数、跨 150+ 任务通用的单一算法」（L8–9、L137–143）。
**方法**：RSSM 世界模型把感官输入编码为离散潜变量 zt，循环状态 ht 预测未来潜表示；actor/critic 完全在隐空间想象轨迹上训练（L288–290），控制不经过像素生成。跨域稳定靠一组鲁棒性技术：symlog 压缩目标尺度（L367–371）、free bits 1 nat 截断 KL 损失（L266–269）、KL balancing 的 stop-gradient 非对称放置（L264–265）、回报百分位归一化 S=EMA(Per95−Per5, 0.99)（L342）、symexp twohot 分布回归（L377、L386）、critic EMA 正则与零初始化（L316–322）。
**实验**：8 域 150+ 任务单配置全胜——Atari 57 任务 Gamer mean 3381% 超 MuZero 3054%（L1604）；ProcGen 66.01 vs PPG 64.89（L1730）；DMLab 100M 步 Human mean capped 71.4% 超 IMPALA 在 1B 步的 66.3%（L1962）；Atari100k Gamer mean 125% 超 IRIS 105%（L2140）；DMC proprio/visual 双双新 SOTA（L531–532、L534–536）；BSuite 66%（L2526）；Minecraft 无人类数据首次拿到钻石，100% agent 在 100M 步内成功（L553–554），1 GPU 9 天 vs VPT 720 GPU 9 天（L597–600）。
**takeaway**：性能主要来自世界模型的无监督重构损失而非任务梯度（L563–566）；模型增大单调提分且降数据需求（L572–573）；其余「泛化」证据集中在超参固定与跨域鲁棒上，隐空间想象的泛化性论文未给理论论证（原文未写）。

## 2. Abstract 全文逐字（L3–17）

"Developing a general algorithm that learns to solve tasks across a wide range of applications has been a fundamental challenge in artificial intelligence. Although current reinforcement learning algorithms can be readily applied to tasks similar to what they have been developed for, configuring them for new application domains requires significant human expertise and experimentation. We present DreamerV3, a general algorithm that outperforms specialized methods across over 150 diverse tasks, with a single configuration. Dreamer learns a model of the environment and improves its behavior by imagining future scenarios. Robustness techniques based on normalization, balancing, and transformations enable stable learning across domains. Applied out of the box, Dreamer is the first algorithm to collect diamonds in Minecraft from scratch without human data or curricula. This achievement has been posed as a significant challenge in artificial intelligence that requires exploring farsighted strategies from pixels and sparse rewards in an open world. Our work allows solving challenging control problems without extensive experimentation, making reinforcement learning broadly applicable."

## 3. 问题与动机

### 3.1 动机

RL 算法迁移到新域需要大量调参，这是应用瓶颈（L137–140）："However, applying reinforcement learning algorithms to sufficiently new tasks—such as moving from video games to robotics tasks—requires substantial effort, expertise, and computational resources for tweaking the hyperparameters of the algorithm13."（L140–141）"This brittleness poses a bottleneck in applying reinforcement learning to new problems and also limits the applicability of reinforcement learning to computationally expensive models or tasks where tuning is prohibitive."（L142–143）"Creating a general algorithm that learns to master new domains without having to be reconfigured has been a central challenge in artificial intelligence and would open up reinforcement learning to a wide range of practical applications."

### 3.2 隐空间想象跨域泛化的论证

论文自述方案（L146–147）："The algorithm is based on the idea of learning a world model that equips the agent with rich perception and the ability to imagine the future 14,15,16."（L147–149）"The world model predicts the outcomes of potential actions, a critic neural network judges the value of each outcome, and an actor neural network chooses actions to reach the best outcomes." 关于「为什么能跨域」论文给出的机制性论证是鲁棒损失/尺度处理（L151–152）："Dreamer overcomes this challenge through a range of robustness techniques based on normalization, balancing, and transformations."（L186–188）"To succeed across domains, all three components need to accommodate different signal magnitudes and robustly balance terms in their objectives. This is challenging as we are not only targeting similar tasks within the same domain but aim to learn across diverse domains with fixed hyperparameters."（L190–191）"This section introduces the world model, critic, and actor along with their robust loss functions, as well as tools for robustly predicting quantities of unknown orders of magnitude." 即：跨域泛化的支撑点是「分量级适配 + 目标函数平衡」，而非世界模型本身的理论保证（原文未写更深层的泛化理论）。隐空间表征的另一功能句（L290–292）："The actor and critic operate on model states st .= {ht, zt} and thus benefit from the Markovian representations learned by the recurrent world model."

## 4. 方法逐机制

### 4.1 RSSM 结构与离散潜变量

（L195–197）"We implement the world model as a Recurrent State-Space Model (RSSM) 24, shown in Figure 3. First, an encoder maps sensory inputs xt to stochastic representations zt. Then, a sequence model with recurrent state ht predicts the sequence of these representations given past actions at−1." 公式 (1)（L239–244）：

```
Sequence model: ht = fφ(ht−1, zt−1, at−1)
Encoder: zt ∼ qφ(zt | ht, xt)
Dynamics predictor: ẑt ∼ pφ(ẑt | ht)
Reward predictor: r̂t ∼ pφ(r̂t | ht, zt)
Continue predictor: ĉt ∼ pφ(ĉt | ht, zt)
Decoder: x̂t ∼ pφ(x̂t | ht, zt)
```

（L233–234）"The concatenation of ht and zt forms the model state from which we predict rewards rt and episode continuation flags ct ∈ {0, 1} and reconstruct the inputs to ensure informative representations:" 潜变量是离散的（L248–250）："The representations are sampled from a vector of softmax distributions and we take straight-through gradients through the sampling step 25,22." 连续潜变量：原文未写（本文只描述 categorical/softmax 采样，未提 continuous 潜变量）。防退化的混入（L283–285）："we parameterize the categorical distributions of the encoder and dynamics predictor as mixtures of 1% uniform and 99% neural network output, making it impossible for them to become deterministic and thus ensuring well-behaved KL losses." 世界模型总损失（L253–258）："the world model parameters φ are optimized end-to-end to minimize the prediction loss Lpred, the dynamics loss Ldyn, and the representation loss Lrep with corresponding loss weights βpred = 1, βdyn = 1, and βrep = 0.1:"

### 4.2 symlog

（L362–366）"L(θ) .= 1/2 (f (x, θ) − symlog(y))2  ŷ .= symexp(f (x, θ))"（公式 8）。（L363–365）"Using the logarithm as transformation would not allow us to predict targets that take on negative values. Therefore, we choose a function from the bi-symmetric logarithmic family 34 that we name symlog as the transformation with the symexp function as its inverse:" symlog(x) = sign(x) ln(|x|+1)，symexp(x) = sign(x)(exp(|x|)−1)（公式 9，L366）。（L367–371）"The symlog function compresses the magnitudes of both large positive and negative values. Unlike the logarithm, it is symmetric around the origin while preserving the input sign. This allows the optimization process to quickly move the network predictions to large values when needed. The symlog function approximates the identity around the origin so that it does not affect learning of targets that are already small enough." 动机（L356–358）："Predicting large targets using a squared loss can lead to divergence whereas absolute and Huber losses7 stagnate learning. On the other hand, normalizing targets based on running statistics5 introduces non-stationarity into the optimization." 用法（L387–389）："Dreamer transforms vector observations using the symlog functions, both for the encoder inputs and the decoder targets and employs the synexp twohot loss for the reward predictor and critic."

### 4.3 free bits

（L266–269）"To avoid a degenerate solution where the dynamics are trivial to predict but fail to contain enough information about the inputs, we employ free bits 26 by clipping the dynamics and representation losses below the value of 1 nat ≈ 1.44 bits. This disables them while they are already minimized well to focus learning on the prediction loss:" 公式 (3)（L271–272）：

```
Ldyn(φ) = max(1, KL[sg(qφ(zt|ht,xt)) ‖ pφ(zt|ht)])
Lrep(φ) = max(1, KL[qφ(zt|ht,xt) ‖ sg(pφ(zt|ht))])
```

（L273–278）"Previous world models require scaling the representation loss differently based on the visual complexity of the environment 21. Complex 3D environments contain details unnecessary for control and thus prompt a stronger regularizer to simplify the representations and make them more predictable. In games with static backgrounds and where individual pixels may matter for the task, a weak regularizer is required to extract fine details. We find that combining free bits with a small representation loss resolves this dilemma, allowing for fixed hyperparameters across domains."

### 4.4 KL balancing

正文未用 "KL balancing" 术语定义该技巧（术语出现在 L976 变更清单与 Figure 6 图例 "No KL balance & free bits"），其机制即式 (3) 中 stop-gradient 的不对称放置（L264–265）："The two losses differ in the stop-gradient operator sg(·) and their loss scale." 即：Ldyn 对后验 qφ stop-gradient、只更新先验 pφ；Lrep 对先验 pφ stop-gradient、只更新后验 qφ。【推断：这正是 DreamerV2 的 KL balancing 设计，本文以 stop-gradient 形式重述。】

### 4.5 imagined policy 训练

（L288–290）"The actor and critic neural networks learn behaviors purely from abstract trajectories of representations predicted by the world model14. For environment interaction, we select actions by sampling from the actor network without lookahead planning." 无 lookahead planning 是重要自述边界（对比 MuZero 树搜索）。想象 rollout（L298–299）："Starting from representations of replayed inputs, the world model and actor generate a trajectory of imagined model states s1:T , actions a1:T , rewards r1:T , and continuation flags c1:T ." Actor 目标（L330–331）："To use a fixed entropy scale of η = 3× 10−4 across domains, we normalize returns to be approximately contained in the interval [0, 1]."（L334–336）"We use the Reinforce estimator31 for both discrete and continuous actions, resulting in the surrogate loss function:" L(θ) = −Σt sg[(Rλt − vψ(st)) / max(1, S)] log πθ(at|st) + η H[πθ(at|st)]（公式 6）。归一化尺度 S（L340–342）："we compute the range from the 5 th to the 95th return percentile over the return batch and smooth out the estimate using an exponential moving average: S .= EMA( Per(Rλt, 95) − Per(Rλt, 5), 0.99 )"（公式 7）。动机（L351–352）："Return normalization with a denominator limit overcomes these challenges, exploring rapidly under sparse rewards and converging to high performance across diverse domains." 超参数表（L999）：Imagination horizon H = 15；注意正文（L295）"beyond the prediction horizon T = 16" 与表 H=15 并存（原文如此，未解释差异）。

### 4.6 回报预测（critic 与 reward head）

Critic 是分布式的（L302–305）："To estimate returns that consider rewards beyond the prediction horizon, we compute bootstrapped λ-returns29 that integrate the predicted rewards and the values. The critic learns to predict the distribution of the return estimates Rλt using the maximum likelihood loss:" L(ψ) = −Σt ln pψ(Rλt | st)，Rλt = rt + γct((1−λ)vt + λRλt+1)，RλT = vT（公式 5）。（L307–310）"we parameterize the critic as categorical distribution with exponentially spaced bins, decoupling the scale of gradients from the prediction targets as described later." bins（L377）："B .= symexp([−20 ... +20])"（公式 10），twohot 损失（L386）："L(θ) .= − twohot(y)T log softmax(f (x, θ))"（公式 11）。稳定化（L316–318）："we stabilize learning by regularizing the critic towards predicting the outputs of an exponentially moving average of its own parameters."（L319–322）"We further noticed that the randomly initialized reward predictor and critic networks at the start of training can result in large predicted rewards that can delay the onset of learning. We thus initialize the output weight matrix of the reward predictor and critic to zeros, which alleviates the problem and accelerates early learning." 双损失（L311–313）："we apply the critic loss both to imagined trajectories with loss scale βval = 1 and to trajectories sampled from the replay buffer with loss scale βrepval = 0.3." 折扣（L293–295）："the actor aims to maximize the return Rt .= Σ∞τ=0 γτ rt+τ with a discount factor γ = 0.997"。

## 8. 叙事关联【推断】（world action models vs Dreamer 类方法的对比基点）

以下 5 条均为【推断】，仅以论文原句为支撑：

1. 【推断】Dreamer 的「想象」严格发生在隐空间、且控制不经过像素生成——这是与「像素级生成/视频生成型世界模型」对比的核心基点。支撑（L193–194）："The world model learns compact representations of sensory inputs through autoencoding 23 and enables planning by predicting future representations and rewards for potential actions."（L288–289）："The actor and critic neural networks learn behaviors purely from abstract trajectories of representations predicted by the world model14." 像素解码只服务表征学习（L175–176）："The inputs are reconstructed to shape the representations."
2. 【推断】Dreamer 无 lookahead planning，是纯反应式 actor 采样；world action models 若强调规划/推理能力，则对比点正是 Dreamer 自述的这一边界。支撑（L289–290）："For environment interaction, we select actions by sampling from the actor network without lookahead planning." 且论文把树搜索视为 EfficientZero 的复杂性来源（L524–526）："EfficientZero 44 holds the state-of-the-art by combining online tree search, prioritized replay, and hyperparameter scheduling, but also resets levels early to increase data diversity, making a comparison difficult."
3. 【推断】Dreamer 的表征是极低维压缩（CNN 编码到 6×6/4×4 再压成离散码），信息瓶颈大；world action models 若做高保真视频/动作生成，其表达粒度的差异可与这一设计对照。支撑（L886–887）："Images are encoded using stride 2 convolutions to resolution 6 × 6 or 4 × 4 and then flattened and are decoded using transposed stride 2 convolutions"；图 4 的 45 帧预测仅作理解示意（L229–231）："the model predicts 45 frames into the future without access to intermediate images. The world model learns an understanding of the underlying structure of each environment."
4. 【推断】Dreamer 的能力上限原文以「首个」而非「高水平」自述：钻石里程碑 12 项、100M 步内 100% agent 拿到过钻石，但钻石 episode 率仅 0.4%，且 Minecraft 得分 9.1/12。支撑（L1232–1233）："At the budget, Dreamer obtains diamonds in 0.4% of episodes, leaving a challenge for future research."（L1041–1043）："we follow the sparse reward structure of the MineRL competition environment18 that rewards 12 milestones leading up to the diamond"。
5. 【推断】「单配置跨 150+ 任务」是 Dreamer 自述的最大卖点，也构成被比较的基准线：任何声称「更 impressive」的世界模型方法，需在（a）超参通用性（b）多域同时达标（c）1 GPU 成本（d）稀疏奖励长程探索 这几个原文强调的维度上与之对照。支撑（L144–145）："We present Dreamer, a general algorithm that outperforms specialized expert algorithms across a wide range of domains while using fixed hyperparameters"（L437–438）："All Dreamer agents are trained on a single Nvidia A100 GPU each, making it reproducible for many research labs."（L177–179，Minecraft 四难点原句见 3.3）。
