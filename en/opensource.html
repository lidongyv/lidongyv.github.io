Finance & quant: exploratory track

I keep a serious personal interest in quantitative trading and in using generative models for factor mining, and I have read the source of the upstream projects below in depth. To be precise: I did not author these repos — I studied, extended and experimented with them locally. I do not currently have a public, self-authored quant repo.

vnpy
An open-source Python quant trading platform. I studied its gateway abstraction and event engine, and how live trading and backtesting can share one event model.

AlphaGPT
Symbolic regression for efficient factor mining on A-shares and crypto. What interests me is how it couples search with representation — structurally the same problem as automated data mining in AD.
