R&D — continuous elements (lane lines)

  • Kept a WYSIWYG strategy for road edges: the model outputs lane lines and edges only, no centre lines; redesigned data cleaning and labelling rules for occluded intersections.
  • Replaced segment-based with query-based: post-processing removed, effort shifted upstream, comparable compute cost, F1 improved 15%.
  • Large recall gains on lateral intersection sides and distant weakly-visible regions; unlike the entry-point strategy it can build lanes directly.
  • A query-based temporal strategy moved tracking into the network; PV refinement reduced the strong dependence on BEV features and introduced a new multi-modal fusion; query grouping enabled case-level fine-tuning.

R&D — discrete elements (arrow, crosswalk, junction)

  • Replaced the two-stage detector with a query-based solution at comparable score and more than 30% higher compute efficiency.
  • Query grouping closed the single-task / multi-task metric gap and enabled one-model multi-task training on same-source data.

Data closed loop
