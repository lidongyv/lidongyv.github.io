About

I am Lidong Yu. I did my master's in computer vision at Beijing Institute of Technology (2016–2019) with Prof. Yunde Jia and Prof. Yuwei Wu, working on stereo matching and monocular depth. From 2019 I worked on autonomous-driving algorithms at UISEE's Nanjing AI Research Institute, NIO, DeepRoute.ai, Horizon Robotics, NVIDIA and Neolix, across perception, tracking, data loops and end-to-end driving.

At Neolix until November 2026. Current interest: landing VLA (vision-language-action) models and world models, and evaluation and data loops for end-to-end autonomous driving.

Open to full-time and advisory roles.
