FoggyStereo: Stereo Matching With Fog Volume Representation

Chengtang Yao, Lidong Yu

Stereo matchingFog volumeAtmospheric scatteringRobustness

Problem

Stereo matching in fog is hard: scattering blurs the image and makes matching ambiguous. Prior work treats fog as noise and discards it before matching. We take the opposite view — the fog itself carries depth cues.

Method

We mine those cues from the rendering viewpoint: reverse the atmospheric scattering process and re-render with the fog inside a selected depth range removed. The quality of the rendered image reflects how correct the selected depth is — the closer to the true depth, the clearer the render. Stacking these renders along the channel axis gives a fog volume, which is fused with the cost volume to rectify the ambiguity that fog introduces.

Results

Improves 10–30% over the SOTA of the time on foggy scenes while staying comparable on clear ones — using a single stereo pair and no extra foggy supervision.
