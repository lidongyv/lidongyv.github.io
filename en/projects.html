Stereo matching with a fog volume representation

Turns fog from noise into a source of information: reverse the atmospheric scattering, re-render with the fog removed at each candidate depth, and stack the renders into a fog volume fused with the cost volume. 10–30% better than the SOTA of the time on foggy scenes, comparable on clear ones.

Neolix Technology (Beijing) Co., Ltd.

Head of AD Algorithms · AD Division II

Owned AD Division II algorithms: perception, end-to-end driving and world models, plus the infrastructure behind them.

  • Used a group query-based model to unify single-task and multi-task training, moving from a multi-head design to a data-friendly single-head multi-task design.

2024.07 — 2025.08

NVIDIA

Principal Engineer · Free IC, Autonomous Vehicle Department

Evolved perception models into a two-stage end-to-end stack and then into one-stage Alpamayo; owned the backbone pretrain / fine-tune / post-training framework and Cosmos world-model pretraining plus render research.

  • Segment-based lane detection (what-you-see-is-what-you-get): optimised super-resolution, better geometric precision, BEV multi-scale fusion, dynamic multi-resolution DCN.
  • Query-based lane detection (static E2E): moved beyond the WYSIWYG formulation, added reasoning, took navi info as input, and covered intersection crossing, split/merge and lane changes as upstream navi decisions.
  • Token-based lane detection: enabled the two-stage → one-stage Alpamayo upgrade by tokenizing all perception elements autoregressively, supporting upstream conditional SA and downstream debugger visualisation.
2023.06 — 2024.07

Horizon Robotics

Head of Static Perception Algorithms · "Saturn V" Project

Light-map PNC solution and the road-understanding module under crowdsourced mapping: replaced segment-based lane detection with query-based and rebuilt the data loop around it.

  • Kept a WYSIWYG strategy for road edges: the model outputs lane lines and edges only, no centre lines; redesigned data cleaning and labelling rules for occluded intersections.
  • Replaced segment-based with query-based: post-processing removed, effort shifted upstream, comparable compute cost, F1 improved 15%.
  • Large recall gains on lateral intersection sides and distant weakly-visible regions; unlike the entry-point strategy it can build lanes directly.
