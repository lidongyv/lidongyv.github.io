    2025.08 — 2026.11

    Neolix Technology (Beijing) Co., Ltd.

    Head of AD Algorithms · AD Division II
    38-person algorithm org · ~8,700 GPUs

    Own the algorithm architecture across perception, end-to-end driving and world models: one group query-based model unifies single-task and multi-task training, perception is refactored to fit E2E, and a two-stage pretrain plus world model takes VLA to production readiness.

    • Unified single-task and multi-task training behind one group query-based model: multi-head → data-friendly single-head, rebalancing task weights at token level (BEV / image tokens) instead of task-loss level.
    • A single query-based model covers object detection, lane detection, occupancy network, E2E traffic-light detection and the navigation model; lane and traffic-light detection became a navi-based static E2E model that gives upstream decisions a strong prior.
    • Two-stage pretraining: LLM distillation from Cosmos / VGGT (CoT, 3D geometry, 3D OD, lane, failsafe, scene recognition) plus perception-model inception; controllable iteration with frozen head / frozen backbone and data added at any time.
    • AR-based trajectory prediction with multi-task fine-tuning, a CoT debugger, multi-modal diffusion and RL post-training; the general world model uses D4RT for unified parallel AR plus 3D insight, with conditional injection from language / layout / trajectory.
    • AI-native and RaaS transformation: VLM auto triage, agent skills driving evaluation and simulation, simulator auto checker, and agent-driven front-end / back-end development and customer service.

    2024.07 — 2025.08

    NVIDIA

    Principal Engineer · Free IC, Autonomous Vehicle Department
    End-to-end driving and world models

    Evolved perception models into a two-stage end-to-end stack and then into one-stage Alpamayo; owned the backbone pretrain / fine-tune / post-training framework and Cosmos world-model pretraining plus render research.

    • Perception → two-stage E2E: lane detection went from segment-based (what-you-see-is-what-you-get, with better super-resolution, geometric precision, BEV multi-scale fusion and dynamic multi-resolution DCN) to query-based (static E2E with navi info, intersections, split/merge and lane changes) and then to token-based.
    • Token-based lane detection unlocked the jump from two-stage to one-stage Alpamayo: all perception elements are tokenized autoregressively, supporting upstream conditional SA and downstream debugger visualization.
    • PNC → one-stage: planning was model-ized, single-mode → multi-mode regression, regression → AR → diffusion; backbones distilled from Cosmos / Qwen / Llama with CoT, 3D geometry and AD multi-task inception.
    • World model: Cosmos pretraining → render research, distilled from VGGT and Qwen; the E2E data closed loop uses offline perception models for auto-labelling and VLM for auto mining.
