CVPR 2026   Finalist

The SA-FARI Dataset: Segment Anything in Footage of Animals for Recognition and Identification

Dante Wasmuht ⋅ Otto Brookes ⋅ Maximilian Schall ⋅ Pablo Palencia ⋅ Christopher Beirne ⋅ Tilo Burghardt ⋅ Majid Mirmehdi ⋅ Hjalmar Kühl ⋅ Mimi Arandjelovic ⋅ Sam Pottie ⋅ Peter Bermant ⋅ Brandon Asheim ⋅ Yi Jin Toh ⋅ Adam Elzinga ⋅ Jason Allan Holmberg ⋅ Andrew Whitworth ⋅ Eleanor Flatt ⋅ Laura Gustafson ⋅ Chaitanya Ryali ⋅ Yuan-Ting Hu ⋅ Baishan Guo ⋅ Andrew Westbury ⋅ Kate Saenko ⋅ Dídac Surís
arXiv PDF ⬇arXiv 摘要Scholar

摘要 Abstract

Automated video analysis is critical for wildlife conservation. A foundational task in this domain is multi-animal tracking (MAT), which underpins applications such as individual re-identification and behavior recognition. However, existing datasets are limited in scale, constrained to a few species, or lack sufficient temporal and geographical diversity -- leaving no suitable benchmark for training general-purpose MAT models applicable across wild animal populations. To address this, we introduce SA-FARI, the largest open-source MAT dataset for wild animals. It comprises 11,609 camera trap videos collected over approximately 10 years (2014-2024) from 741 locations across 4 continents, spanning 99 species categories. Each video is exhaustively annotated culminating in $\sim$46 hours of densely annotated footage containing 16,224 masklet identities and 942,702 individual bounding boxes, segmentation masks, and species labels. Alongside the task-specific annotations, we publish anonymized camera trap locations for each video. Finally, we present comprehensive benchmarks on SA-FARI using state-of-the-art vision-language models for detection and tracking, including SAM 3, evaluated with both species-specific and generic animal prompts. We also compare against vision-only methods developed specifically for wildlife analysis. SA-FARI is the first large-scale dataset to combine high species diversity, multi-region coverage, and high-quality spatio-temporal annotations, offering a new foundation for advancing generalizable multi-animal tracking in the wild. The dataset is available at [ANONYMIZED]

📝 精读笔记

灵魂一句
迄今最大的野生动物多目标追踪(MAT)开源数据集：11,609 段相机陷阱视频(2014–2024 约 10 年)、741 个采样点、横跨 4 大洲、99 个物种类别，~46 小时密集标注，16,224 条 masklet 身份 + 942,702 个 bbox/分割掩码/物种标签，且首次大规模提供人工核验的时空分割掩码(经 SAM 3 data engine + 人工校正)。配 SAM 3 等 VLM 的检测/追踪 benchmark：在 SA-FARI 上微调让 SAM 3 比基线涨 20+ 点 HOTA(cgF1 +32.9)。
问题 & 动机
生物多样性正以空前速度衰退，保护亟需自动化视频分析(物种/个体/行为)，而相机陷阱(CT)产生的数据量早已超过人工处理能力。MAT(multi-animal tracking)是个体 re-ID、行为识别的底层前置，但现有数据集四大短板：① MAT 专用集(AnimalTrack/GMOT/TAO)规模太小(<1h)、无生态传感器footage；② 较大的也只覆盖 ≤5 物种、≤10h；③ UAV 野外集多为单地点、地理/时间跨度窄；④ 多数只给 bbox，分割掩码常是自动生成、未人工后处理。→ 缺一个能训“通用 MAT 模型”的 benchmark。
核心方法（数据集构建，非算法）
① 数据采集：4 大洲(中非/南美/中美/南欧)、7 家保护机构、741 独立采样点；多品牌 CT，离地 0.3–2m(另 2,790 段哥斯达黎加树冠 arboreal CT 离地 8–24m)；运动触发、夜间 IR 闪光；分辨率 320×194~2688×1234、10–60 FPS(多 30)、时长 0.5–90s(多 15s)；含音频(本文未用)。② 物种标注：本地专家给俗名→2–4 名标注者独立确认；附拉丁名 + 完整分类阶元(界门纲目科属种)。③ 分割标注(SAM 3 data engine, 6fps)：SAM 3 自动 pseudo-标注全部 video-species 对 → 按 IoU 去重 → 人工核验(查可见性 + 第二标注者用 online SAM 2 在环修正 + exhaustivity 检查)；bbox 由 mask 自动导出。
关键口径 / 评测指标（无核心算法公式）
规模(Tab.2)：总 11,609 视频 / 2,747 min / 99 物种 / 16,224 masklet / 942,702 标注 / 741 站点；train 10,776、test 833，train/test 相机点零重叠。物种构成：67.7% 哺乳类、27% 鸟类、4% 爬行、1% 两栖；top-3=蜘蛛猴/领西貒/刺豚鼠；均 1.4 masklet/视频(最多 14)。“challenging” 判据：masklet 平均相邻帧 IoU <0.7(动得多) 或 遮挡次数 ≥2。评测指标：cgF1(classification-gated F1)、pHOTA(phrase-based HOTA，HOTA=√(DetA·AssA) 的开放词表版，分 Total/Det/Ass)、TETA(Track Every Thing Accuracy)、IDF1、HOTA。负样本增广：每个 video-species 对配 1 个同组 hard-negative + 1 个跨组 easy-negative(共 29 组)。
关键结果
物种特定 prompt(Tab.3, cgF1/pHOTA/TETA)：SAM 3(14.0/48.5/39.6) > LLMDet+SAM3-TR(2.6/41.3/30.4) > GLEE(−0.2/7.5/22.0)；加 SA-FARI 训 SAM3(SA-FARI) 39.0/63.1/52.1，微调 SAM3 FT(SA-FARI) 46.9/68.1/58.7(vs 原 SAM3 +32.9 cgF1 / +19.6 pHOTA / +19.1 TETA，≈3× 提升)。物种无关 “animal” query(Tab.4)：SAM3(SA-FARI) IDF1 71.1 / HOTA 64.4，超次优 +23.9 IDF1 / +18.9 HOTA。子集(Tab.5)：大 mask 比小 mask pHOTA +29.2；challenging 追踪更难(pHOTA-Ass −9.3)。
亮点
① 规模/多样性碾压前作(5× 标注时长、2× 物种数)；② 唯一大规模人工核验时空分割掩码的野生 MAT 集；③ 与 SAM 3 data engine 深度绑定(自动 + human-in-the-loop)；④ 精心的 site-disjoint 划分 + long-tail open-world(部分物种只在 test，如 Saki monkey)；⑤ 含匿名地理位置、音频等可扩展模态。
疑问 / 待查
① 摘要数据集地址被匿名化([ANONYMIZED])，正文给的是 conservationxlabs.com/SA-FARI——以发布版为准；② SAM 3 本身的 PCS 评测协议细节与训练数据靠引用 SAM 3 论文[12]，需查该文；③ 标注 6fps、视频原生最高 60fps，时间分辨率折损对快速运动 masklet 的影响原文未量化。

📖 逐段精读 · 笔记

① Abstract（摘要）— 逐句精讲
原文骨架（verbatim）："Automated video analysis is critical for wildlife conservation. A foundational task in this domain is multi-animal tracking (MAT), which underpins applications such as individual re-identification and behavior recognition." 痛点："existing datasets are limited in scale, constrained to a few species, or lack sufficient temporal and geographical diversity – leaving no suitable benchmark for training general-purpose MAT models." 方案："we introduce SA-FARI, the largest open-source MAT dataset for wild animals."
规模逐项(verbatim)："It comprises 11,609 camera trap videos collected over approximately 10 years (2014-2024) from 741 locations across 4 continents, spanning 99 species categories." 标注量："∼46 hours of densely annotated footage containing 16,224 masklet identities and 942,702 individual bounding boxes, segmentation masks, and species labels." 收尾定位："SA-FARI is the first large-scale dataset to combine high species diversity, multi-region coverage, and high-quality spatio-temporal annotations." benchmark 用 SAM 3，分 "species-specific and generic animal prompts" 两套设置。
📚 背景知识 A：camera trap / MAT / tracklet vs masklet
camera trap(CT，相机陷阱)：野外布设的运动触发相机，是生态学主力 in-situ 传感器；原文动机 "the growing amount of data collected by in-situ sensors – such as camera traps (CTs) – which now vastly exceed the processing capacity of human experts"。MAT(multi-animal tracking)= multi-object tracking(MOT) 在动物域的特例。本文术语约定(verbatim)："we use 'tracklet' to refer to both bounding box or mask sequences … 'Masklets' refer specifically to tracklets that contain mask information, i.e., sequences where each element is a segmentation mask rather than just a bounding box." → 一个 masklet ≈ 一只被持续追踪的动物个体。
📚 背景知识 B：SAM 2 / SAM 3 / data engine
SAM 2=对图像/视频做 promptable 分割 + memory-based 追踪。SAM 3(本文既用其 data engine 标注、又作 benchmark 模型) 原文定义："extends the Segment Anything Model 2 (SAM 2) to support open-vocabulary, text-based promptable segmentation and tracking. It can segment and track all instances of a user-specified concept across images and videos, using a DETR-style detector and a SAM 2-style memory-based tracker with a shared vision-language backbone." SAM 3 "is trained on a large, diverse dataset … annotated with millions of unique concept phrases, collected via a human- and AI-in-the-loop pipeline." SA-FARI 的掩码即由 SAM 3 data engine 自动初标 + 人工修。
📚 背景知识 C：追踪/分割指标（HOTA 家族，含公式）
HOTA(Higher Order Tracking Accuracy) 把性能拆成检测准确 DetA 与关联准确 AssA：\[ \mathrm{HOTA}=\sqrt{\mathrm{DetA}\cdot\mathrm{AssA}} \] pHOTA(phrase-based HOTA)："Each video–noun phrase pair is treated as a unique sample, enabling class-agnostic evaluation"，分 Total/Det/Ass。TETA(Track Every Thing Accuracy)："consists of three parts: a localization score, an association score, and a classification score"。cgF1(classification-gated F1)："combines localization quality (positive micro F1, pmF1) and classification ability (image-level Matthews correlation coefficient, IL MCC)"，记 \( \mathrm{cgF1}\sim \mathrm{pmF1}\times \mathrm{IL\text{-}MCC} \)，为大标签空间设计、补 AP 之短。IDF1=身份保持准确度(平衡 precision/recall 并惩罚 identity switch)。
② §1 Introduction — problem → solution → why 闭环
problem：生物多样性以 "orders of magnitude faster than at any time in the last tens of millions of years" 的速度衰退；MAT 是 re-ID/行为识别的前置(提供时空动物表征，且 "help to prevent shortcut learning of spurious background cues, which are known to hinder out-of-distribution (OOD) generalisation")。但现有集四短板：(i) MAT 专用集 "lack scale (less than one hour of annotated footage)" 且无生态传感器 footage；(ii) 较大集 "focus on a small number of species – no more than 5 … no more than 10 hours"；(iii) UAV 野外集 "generally collected from a single location … narrow geographic range and short temporal window"；(iv) "most datasets provide localisation only via bounding boxes … often generated automatically without manual post-processing, leaving annotation quality uncertain."
solution + why：SA-FARI 把多机构 11,609 段 CT 视频合成一个大公开集，穷尽标注 bbox/mask/物种/个体 masklet。原文量化优势："This is 5× greater in total annotated duration and 2× broader in species diversity than any existing MAT dataset to date." 且 "the only large-scale dataset to include high-quality manually annotated segmentation masks for wild multi-animal tracking." why 分割掩码重要：掩码(coat pattern/body shape/pose)利于细粒度个体/物种/行为区分、抑制背景 shortcut、利 OOD 泛化——这是“做大”之外的质量护城河。
③ Contribution（verbatim）
原文："We present SA-FARI, by far the largest open-source MAT dataset for wild animals … 11,609 camera trap videos collected over 10 years from 741 locations across 4 continents." 关键 benchmark 结论一句："Our results show that training on SA-FARI leads to over 20-point gains in HOTA metrics, underscoring the value of our large-scale, diverse annotations."
④ §2 Related Work — 三类既有数据集 & 其局限
(1) MAT benchmark：AnimalTrack[53]=58 段 YouTube、10 物种、1,927 tracklet；GMOT[2]=16 段、3 物种；TAO[17]=429 段、51 类、均 2 动物/视频，BURST[1] 在 TAO 上加掩码。共病："these datasets lack in-situ footage from ecological sensors, and therefore do not capture animals in natural environments." (2) Wild MAT(多 UAV)：BuckTales[34]=12 段黑羚 UAV(单 lekking 点)、BaboonLand[18]=18 段狒狒、KABR[26]=~10h 斑马/长颈鹿 UAV、WildLive[16]=22 段 drone 且掩码自动生成。共病："limited to a narrow geographic and temporal scope … rely heavily on automated annotations without manual correction." (3) 行为/Re-ID 附带 MAT：LoTE[30]=28k 图 11 物种、MammAlps[20]=6K tracklet 5 物种(>90% 是鹿)、PanAf500[8]=500 段黑猩猩/大猩猩 CT、CCR[4]=>5k 脸+12k 身体轨迹。
⑤ §3 数据采集（Data Collection）
4 大洲(Central Africa / South America / Mesoamerica / Southern Europe)、7 家保护机构、741 独立采样点；多品牌 CT(Browning/Bushnell/Reconyx/…) "usually 0.3m to 2m from the ground"，另 "2,790 videos from Costa Rica were recorded by cameras set up in tree crowns (arboreal camera traps) between 8 and 24m"；运动触发、夜间 IR flash "invisible to the species of interest"；分辨率 320×194~2688×1234、10–60 FPS(多 30)、时长 0.5–90s(多 15s)；跨度约 10 年(2014-2024)；"All videos within the dataset contain at least one animal"；含音频但本文未用。
⑥ §3 物种标注（Species Annotation）
本地团队专家给俗名(如 fox)→"2-4 independent annotators then confirmed the common name"；同视频多物种在时空 tracklet 标注时按个体赋独立俗名消歧。附拉丁名(如 Vulpes vulpes) + 完整分类阶元 "Kingdom: Animalia - Phylum: Chordata - Class: Mammalia - Order: Carnivora - Family: Canidae - Genus: Vulpes - Species: Vulpes vulpes"，至少到 family、通常到 species。每段附日期时间戳 + 匿名采样点字符串。
⑦ §3 分割掩码标注（SAM 3 data engine, 6fps）
自动阶段："SAM 3 is used to pseudo-annotate all video-species category pairs, generating initial masks … These masklets are then deduplicated based on their Intersection over Union (IoU)"(掩码重叠 \( \mathrm{IoU}=\frac{|A\cap B|}{|A\cup B|} \) 去冗余)。人工阶段：第一标注者查可见性(排除过糊/难辨，如混在 flock 中)；第二标注者 "using online SAM 2 in the loop to improve or add masklets"；最后 exhaustivity 检查确保所有 masklet 都标了。bbox 不人工标："they are trivially derived from the segmentation masks by computing their spatial limits."
⑧ §3 划分 / 负样本增广 / test 子集
train/test 划分：test ≤1,000 视频、最大化物种多样性，且 "ensuring that all videos from the same camera trap are assigned to the same split"(站点零泄漏)；按相机“物种类别/视频”比贪心选点。负样本增广：物种归 29 组，每个 video-species 对配 1 个同组 hard-negative + 1 个跨组 easy-negative(都确保不在该视频)——靠 exhaustive 标注才可行。test 五子集：challenging(106)/night(598)/multi-masklet(136)/large-masklet(211)/small-masklet(212)。challenging 判据："the average inter-frame IoU of the masklet being < 0.7" 或 "the number of occlusions is ≥2"；large/small 子集按平均 masklet size 的 \(>75\%\) / \(<25\%\) 分位，size \(\in[0,1]\) 为帧级 mask 占图比的平均。

⑪ §4 Benchmark — 两套设置
(1) 物种特定 prompt：VLM 收物种名，按 SAM 3 的 video promptable concept segmentation(PCS) 协议定位+追踪该物种全部实例。三模型：GLEE[51](开放词表检测追踪)、LLMDet[19](仅检测，外接追踪器)、SAM 3。SAM 3 再加两配置：参训(SA-FARI 按比例混入) 与显式微调，记 SAM 3 / SAM 3 (SA-FARI) / SAM 3 FT (SA-FARI)。(2) 物种无关：物种名换通用 query "animal"，对比 SAM3(SA-FARI) vs MegaDetector(MD)+ByteTrack/OCSort/BoostSort++。注意 "SAM 3 models trained on SA-FARI were not exposed to the 'animal' query during training; this prompt is used only at inference time."

⑭ §5 Conclusion + 局限/扩展
结论：SA-FARI 以 "over 11,000 camera trap videos from over 700 sampling sites … 99 species categories and sightings spanning a decade" 提供空前规模，且首次大规模人工核验时空分割掩码(16k+ masklet)。关键发现："even SOTA models perform poorly in this domain without large-scale, richly annotated collections like SA-FARI"，微调 SAM 3 实现 "a three-fold improvement over its baseline"。扩展方向(verbatim)："The integrated audio streams present a compelling opportunity for future multi-modal model development that leverage vocalizations to enhance detection, species classification and tracking robustness in the wild." 另建议 "Future data integration should prioritize additional ecoregions to capture a wider array of species and mitigate geographic bias." 即加 body pose / depth / 自然语言描述等模态，补更多生态区减地理偏置。
⑮ 疑问 / 局限 [推测]
① arXiv 版摘要数据集地址被匿名([ANONYMIZED])，正文/项目页给 conservationxlabs.com/SA-FARI——以发布版为准；② SAM 3 的 PCS 协议、训练数据、cgF1/pHOTA 精确算法靠引用 SAM 3 论文[12]，需查原文；③ 标注仅 6fps 而视频原生最高 60fps，时间下采样对快速运动/短暂出现 masklet 的影响未量化[推测]；④ benchmark 强绑 SAM 3 生态(既当标注器又当被测模型)，可能存在自指偏置[推测]；⑤ 地理/物种长尾极重(29 类占 90%)，尾部物种评测样本稀少、指标方差大[推测]。
📌 全文一页总结（≥3 takeaway）
problem：野生动物 MAT 缺一个大规模、多物种、多地点、含人工核验分割掩码的 benchmark，训不出通用 MAT 模型。method(数据集)：合并 4 大洲 741 点的 11,609 段相机陷阱视频，穷尽标注 99 物种 / 16,224 masklet / 942,702 个 bbox+mask；SAM 3 data engine 自动初标 + online SAM 2 人工在环核验；site-disjoint 划分 + hard/easy 负样本增广 + 5 个难度子集。experiment：微调 SAM 3 后 cgF1/pHOTA/TETA = 46.9/68.1/58.7，比原 SAM3 +32.9/+19.6/+19.1(≈3×)；通用 “animal” query 也以 IDF1 71.1/HOTA 64.4 大幅领先 MegaDetector+经典追踪器。
takeaway：① 规模 + 多样性 + 人工掩码质量是训“跨种群通用”野生 MAT 的三件套，缺一不可；② 即便 SAM 3 这类 SOTA VLM 在野外也需域内数据微调才好用(domain-specific data 价值被实测确认)；③ 小目标 / 遮挡运动是最大难点(small mask 比 large 低 29.2 pHOTA、challenging 关联 −9.3)，为后续数据采集与建模指明方向；④ 数据集预留 音频 / pose / depth 等模态，是后续多模态野保模型的接口。
