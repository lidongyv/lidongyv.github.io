CVPR 2026   Finalist

SmokeSVD: Smoke Reconstruction from A Single View via Progressive Novel View Synthesis and Refinement with Diffusion Models

Chen Li ⋅ Shanshan Dong ⋅ Sheng Qiu ⋅ Jianmin Han ⋅ Yibo Zhao ⋅ Zan Gao ⋅ Taku Komura ⋅ Kemeng Huang
arXiv PDF ⬇arXiv 摘要Scholar

摘要 Abstract

Reconstructing dynamic fluids from sparse views is a long-standing and challenging problem, due to the severe lack of 3D information from insufficient view coverage. While several pioneering approaches have attempted to address this issue using differentiable rendering or novel view synthesis, they are often limited by time-consuming optimization under ill-posed conditions. We propose SmokeSVD, an efficient and effective framework to progressively reconstruct dynamic smoke from a single video by integrating the generative capabilities of diffusion models with physically guided consistency optimization. Specifically, we first propose a physically guided side-view synthesizer based on diffusion models, which explicitly incorporates velocity field constraints to generate spatio-temporally consistent side-view images frame by frame, significantly alleviating the ill-posedness of single-view reconstruction. Subsequently, we iteratively refine novel-view images and reconstruct 3D density fields through a progressive multi-stage process that renders and enhances images from increasing viewing angles, generating high-quality multi-view sequences. Finally, we estimate fine-grained density and velocity fields via differentiable advection by leveraging the Navier-Stokes equations. Our approach supports re-simulation and downstream applications while achieving superior reconstruction quality and computational efficiency compared to state-of-the-art methods.

📝 精读笔记

灵魂一句
从「单视角视频」重建动态 3D 烟雾(密度场+速度场)。单视角极度 ill-posed(没 3D 信息)。SmokeSVD 三步：① 物理引导的侧视图合成器 SvDiff——用 diffusion 逐帧从正视图生成时空一致的 90° 侧视图，把速度场约束(无散+平滑)注入去噪；② 渐进式新视角精化 NvRef——从近到远逐步 render+refine(最多 16 视角)、coarse→fine 重建密度场；③ 用可微 advection(Navier-Stokes)联合估细密度+速度+inflow。质量与效率双超 SOTA。
问题 & 动机
原文：多视角采集烟雾对非专业者不现实，单/稀疏视角重建价值大但严重 ill-posed。现有稀疏视角法(物理+几何先验)耗时；单视角(GlobTrans 可微渲染)贵；近期 diffusion 生成新视角(FluidNexus)缓解 ill-posed 但两难：(1) “limited multi-view consistency”(diffusion 出图低质不一致)，(2) “insufficient incorporation of physical priors” 难引导复杂烟雾动力学与 inflow。
核心方法 + 关键公式
① SvDiff(§3.2)：扩散逐帧合成侧视图，条件 c^t=w^t_∠0° ⊕ w^{t−1}_∠90° ⊕ w^{t−2}_∠90°(Eq.1)，L_noise=∥ε−ε_θ(w^t_∠90°,c^t,s)∥²(Eq.2)。multi-frame 训练(用上一帧「估出的 clean 图」当条件，Eq.3)减累积误差。当噪声步 s ＜ T_Q 启 guidance：visual L_img、velocity L_vel=∥∇·u∥²+∥∇u∥²(无散+平滑，Eq.4)、spatial L_sp=∥H(w_c,∠90°)−H(w_∠0°)∥²(H=按宽度行求和，Eq.5)；L_SvDiff=λ·各项(Eq.6)。
② NvRef(§3.3)：density generator Gρ=UNet3+(3D conv)，ρ_r^t=Gρ(多视图 concat)(Eq.7)，loss=密度 L2 + 输入视角渲染 L2 + 未知视角渲染 L2(Eq.8)；＜16 视图=coarse Gc_ρ、=16=fine Gf_ρ。NvRef 精化模糊新视图：res=NvRef(相邻 ±β 视图 ⊕ 当前视图 ⊕ 降采样前两帧 refined)，w_f=res+w_r(Eq.9)；16 视角分 clear/near/mid/far，从近到远渐进。
③ 物理(supp)：Gu 从相邻两帧密度估速度，可微 advection A 按 transport eq 输运密度，frame-by-frame 估 inflow；Navier-Stokes 无散约束。
关键结果
ScalarFlow(Table 1)：GlobTrans 感知最佳(SSIM 0.9975/PSNR 40.16)但 ＞30h；Ours SSIM 0.9868/PSNR 38.08/LPIPS 0.0223 仅 15min；NGT 5min 但质量低。vs FluidNexus(Table 2)：input RMSE 0.0172/SSIM 0.9764 远超(novel 略逊但免阈值敏感)。vs NeuSmoke(Table 3)：PSNR 30.04 vs 26.50。synthetic(Table 4)：PSNR 28.13 vs NGT/PICT/PINF 13~16。消融：SvDiff 去 threshold/vel/grad/divergence 全降；SvDiff-F4(4 次前向)、16 视角最佳。
亮点
① 用「显式合成侧视图 + 渐进精化」破单视角歧义，而非一次性多视角 diffusion(后者 shape-appearance 模糊)；② 把速度无散/inflow 等物理约束嵌进 diffusion 去噪与重建、保时序一致(去 flicker)；③ multi-frame 训练用「估出的 clean 图」当条件，专治 diffusion 长序列累积误差；④ 兼容 3DGS、支持 re-simulation/加障碍物/外力等下游控制。
疑问 / 边界
① 限制(原文)：假设干净背景 + 稳定光照，复杂背景/变光会退化；② 强依赖初始侧视图精度——早期错误会传播；③ 仅在合成+受控真实集评测，户外多样烟雾未验；④ 高分辨率/长序列计算仍可观；⑤ 仅灰度烟雾，不处理彩色烟、固体障碍、复杂环境交互。

① Abstract（摘要）— 逐句精讲
原文逐句：
1) “Reconstructing dynamic fluids from sparse views is a long-standing and challenging problem, due to the severe lack of 3D information from insufficient view coverage.” —— 稀疏视角流体重建之难，根因是视角覆盖不足导致 3D 信息严重缺失（ill-posed 的来源）。
2) “While several pioneering approaches have attempted to address this issue using differentiable rendering or novel view synthesis, they are often limited by time-consuming optimization under ill-posed conditions.” —— 前作（可微渲染 / 新视角合成）受困于在 ill-posed 条件下的耗时优化。
3) “We propose SmokeSVD, an efficient and effective framework to progressively reconstruct dynamic smoke from a single video by integrating the generative capabilities of diffusion models with physically guided consistency optimization.” —— 一句话定位：diffusion 生成能力 + 物理引导一致性优化，逐步从单视频重建动态烟雾。
4) “a physically guided side-view synthesizer based on diffusion models, which explicitly incorporates velocity field constraints to generate spatio-temporally consistent side-view images frame by frame” —— 机制①：物理引导侧视图合成器，显式注入速度场约束，逐帧产时空一致侧视图。
5) “we iteratively refine novel-view images and reconstruct 3D density fields through a progressive multi-stage process that renders and enhances images from increasing viewing angles” —— 机制②：渐进多阶段精化新视角 + 重建 3D 密度场，视角从小到大递增。
6) “we estimate fine-grained density and velocity fields via differentiable advection by leveraging the Navier-Stokes equations” —— 机制③：可微 advection + NS 方程估细密度/速度场。
7) “supports re-simulation and downstream applications while achieving superior reconstruction quality and computational efficiency.”
一句话抓 Abstract：单视角→先用物理引导 diffusion 造侧视图、再从近到远渐进补视角并重建密度，最后用 NS 可微 advection 拿到能 re-simulation 的密度+速度场，质量与效率双超 SOTA。
📚 背景知识（外部，非本文原文）
① 烟雾/流体重建（逆问题）：从图像反推 3D 密度场 \( \rho \)（每体素烟浓度）与速度场 \( \mathbf{u} \)。多视角易、单视角 ill-posed（同一张投影对应无数 3D 体）。
② Navier-Stokes / transport（本文 supp 公式）：不可压流体 \[ \frac{\partial \mathbf{u}}{\partial t}+(\mathbf{u}\cdot\nabla)\mathbf{u}=-\frac{\nabla p}{\rho}+\nu\nabla^2\mathbf{u}+\mathbf{f}, \qquad \nabla\cdot\mathbf{u}=0 \] 前者动量方程，后者质量守恒（不可压=无散）；烟雾粘度 \( \nu \approx 0 \)。密度按 transport equation \[ \frac{\partial \rho}{\partial t}+\mathbf{u}\cdot\nabla\rho = 0 \] 被速度场输运。
③ advection（平流）：用速度场把密度场“搬运”到下一帧；differentiable advection \( \mathcal{A} \) 是可微版，能反传梯度去约束速度估计。
④ inflow（入流源）\( \rho_{in} \)：烟雾持续从源头涌入；原文 “underestimating the inflow will lead to an inability to fill the smoke volume ... overestimating can cause obvious instability”。
⑤ DDPM / classifier-free guidance：前向加噪 \( x_s=\sqrt{\bar\alpha_s}x_0+\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_s}\epsilon \)、反向去噪 \( \min\|\epsilon-\epsilon_\theta(x_s,s)\|^2 \) 生成；本文把它改成逐帧条件生成侧视图，并用 DDIM 加速采样。
⑥ UNet3+ [17]：全尺度跳连的 UNet（原用于医学分割），本文把 2D conv 扩成 3D conv 当密度生成器 \( G_\rho \) 与精化器 NvRef。
⑦ 对比基线：GlobTrans/Franz [8]（可微渲染优化，准但 >30h）、PINF [5]（hybrid neural field）/PICT [35]（neural characteristic trajectory field，NeRF 类，稀疏视角易过平滑）、NGT（神经网络估单次运动，快）、FluidNexus [10]（多视角 diffusion 再重建）、NeuSmoke [25]（neural transportation fields）。
② §1 Introduction — problem（问题）
原文：“it is cumbersome and sometimes impractical for non-specialist to capture multi-view images of smoke phenomena in non-laboratory environments” —— 多视角采集烟雾对非专业者不现实，故单/稀疏视角重建价值大。
三难并列（原文）：“Existing solutions for sparse-view fluid capture integrate physically-based and geometric priors but are time-consuming”（稀疏视角法慢）；“Franz et al. introduced physical priors via differentiable rendering, but remains computationally expensive”（单视角可微渲染贵）；diffusion 新视角法虽缓解 ill-posed 但 “faces two challenges: (1) limited multi-view consistency, where diffusion models produce low-quality inconsistent images, and (2) insufficient incorporation of physical priors to guide generative models for complex smoke dynamics and external inflows.”
③ §1 Introduction — solution（核心解法闭环）
原文：“we first synthesize side-view sequences from front-view input using diffusion models guided by spatial and temporal priors. We then progressively generate novel views from near to far. Each iteration reconstructs a coarse 3D density field, then refines novel views using differentiable rendering and UNet3+ for visual fidelity and temporal coherence. Finally, we reconstruct fine-grained density and velocity fields, and infer inflow states to support downstream applications.”
指路：front-view → 造 side-view → coarse 密度 → 渐进 render+refine 新视角 → fine 密度 + 速度 + inflow → re-simulation。Intro 这段就是 Method 的自然语言版，闭环已自包含。
④ §1 — why（vs 一次性多视角 diffusion）+ 三大贡献
why（原文）：“Unlike recent sparse-view methods [FluidNexus] that first generate multi-view images then reconstruct 3D, leading to shape-appearance ambiguity from insufficient consistency, we advocate a multi-stage strategy cyclically utilizing 2D diffusion synthesis, spatio-temporal refinement, and coarse/fine-grained 3D reconstruction. This exploits both high-quality 2D diffusion outputs and 3D volumetric consistency.” —— 关键洞见：循环交替 2D diffusion 与 3D 体一致性，而非一次性多视角生成，兼得高质 2D 输出与跨视角一致性、且计算量小。
三大贡献（原文 bullets 摘要）：① “a novel and efficient smoke reconstruction framework from a single view” 含 multi-stage 合成/精化 + coarse/fine 3D 重建，可快速推 velocity + inflow 支持 re-simulation；② side-view synthesizer “incorporate 3D predicted density and velocity fields as physical guidance into the denoising process”；③ novel view refinement “achieves a better balance between computational efficiency and reconstruction robustness” 相比直接多视角 diffusion。
⑤ §2 Related Work — 方法定位
Fluid reconstruction：传统靠专用设备（Schlieren、structured light、light field probes）；神经渲染 PINF/HyFluid/PICT “faces challenges capturing high-frequency information from sparse views, often producing over-smooth results”（稀疏视角→过平滑，本文要解决）。单视角 GlobTrans 用 “strict differentiable physical priors”、FluidNexus 用合成多视角视频但 “consistency issues may exist among viewpoints”。
NVS with 2D diffusion：Zero-1-to-3/3DiM 拼条件，Zero123++/MVDream/Consistent123/ConsistNet/ViVid-1-to-3 各自增强一致性，但 “existing methods cannot be directly applied to smoke due to its complex physical properties”——本文针对烟雾半透明 + 复杂动力学定制物理引导。
⑥ §3.1 Method — pipeline 总览
原文：给 T 帧单视频，记为 front-view \( w^t_{\angle 0^\circ} \)（\( \alpha \) = 相对正视的偏转角）。流程：SvDiff 造 side-view \( w^t_{p,\angle 90^\circ} \) → coarse 密度生成器 \( G^c_\rho \) 出 rough 密度 \( \rho_{r,c} \) → 沿水平面渐进转相机 render 新视角（如 \( w^t_{r,\angle 45^\circ}, w^t_{r,\angle 135^\circ} \)）+ NvRef 逐帧精化 → fine 密度生成器 \( G^f_\rho \) 出 \( \rho_{r,f} \)，并经可微 advection \( \mathcal{A} \) 联合估速度 \( \mathbf{u} \) 与 inflow \( \rho_{in} \)。原文：“Benefiting from 3D spatial distribution constraint from \( \rho_c \) and temporal-spatial correlation from UNet3+, NvRef produces multi-view consistent images.”
⑦ §3.2 SvDiff 机制① — 条件 + multi-frame 训练（Eq.1-3）
条件构造（Eq.1）：\[ c^t = w^t_{\angle 0^\circ} \oplus w^{t-1}_{\angle 90^\circ} \oplus w^{t-2}_{\angle 90^\circ} \]（\( \oplus \)=concat，用正视图 + 前两帧侧视图当条件）；初始帧 \( t<2 \) 另训一个以 \( c^0=w^0_{\angle 0^\circ}\oplus w^1_{\angle 0^\circ} \)。
去噪损失（Eq.2）：\[ \mathcal{L}_{noise}=\|\epsilon-\epsilon_\theta(w^t_{\angle 90^\circ},c^t,s)\|^2 \]
累积误差问题（原文）：“during inference, SvDiff uses previously synthesized frames as input conditions, which progressively accumulates errors over time.”
multi-frame 训练（Eq.3）：从噪声估 clean 图 \[ w_{\angle 90^\circ}\approx w_{c,\angle 90^\circ}=\frac{w_{s,\angle 90^\circ}-\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_s}\,\epsilon_\theta}{\sqrt{\bar\alpha_s}} \] 原文：“our multi-frame training performs multiple forward processes per batch. In each forward process, SvDiff estimates a clean image from the noisy image and uses it as condition for the next forward process.” —— 训练时也用自己估出的 clean 图当下一次前向的条件，专治长序列累积误差（见 Alg.1）。
⑧ §3.2 SvDiff 机制② — 三大 guidance loss（Eq.4-6）
门控（原文）：设阈值 \( T_Q \)：“if \( s \ge T_Q \), the noise level is too high to extract meaningful physical information ... so the guidance is disabled; otherwise ... activated.” 噪声太大时关 guidance。
Visual：\( \mathcal{L}_{img}=\|x^i_0-\hat{x}^i_0\|^2 \)（预测 clean 图 vs GT，L2，i=multi-frame 迭代序号）。
Velocity（Eq.4）：先用 \( G_\rho \) 重建 coarse 密度 \( \rho^i_{r,c}=G_\rho(w^{i+t}_{\angle 0^\circ},w^{i+t}_{c,\angle 90^\circ}) \)、再用 \( G_u \) 估速度 \( \mathbf{u}^{i-1} \)，约束 \[ \mathcal{L}_{vel}=\|\nabla\cdot\mathbf{u}^{i-1}\|^2+\|\nabla\mathbf{u}^{i-1}\|^2 \] 第一项 “enforces incompressibility”（无散），第二项 “promotes smoothness, preventing temporal artifacts”（平滑去 flicker）。
Spatial（Eq.5）：\[ \mathcal{L}_{sp}=\|H(w_{c,\angle 90^\circ})-H(w_{\angle 0^\circ})\|^2 \] \( H() \)=按宽度方向对每行求和，把 \( H\times W \) 图压成 \( H\times 1 \) 向量，比对正/侧视空间分布。
总损失（Eq.6）：\[ \mathcal{L}_{SvDiff}=\lambda_{noise}\mathcal{L}_{noise}+\lambda_{img}\mathcal{L}_{img}+\lambda_{sp}\mathcal{L}_{sp}+\lambda_{vel}\mathcal{L}_{vel} \]
⑨ §3.3 机制③ — 密度生成器 \( G_\rho \)（Eq.7-8）
密度估计（Eq.7）：\[ \rho^t_r=\mathcal{G}_\rho(I^t),\quad I^t=w^t_{\angle 0^\circ}\oplus w^t_{p,\angle 90^\circ}\oplus\cdots \] \( G_\rho \) 用 UNet3+ 架构、2D conv 扩成 3D conv。
损失（Eq.8）：\[ \mathcal{L}_{\mathcal{G}_\rho}=\lambda_\rho\|\rho^t_r-\rho^t\|^2+\lambda_{in}\sum_{\alpha\in\mathbb{A}}\|\mathcal{R}(\rho^t_r,\alpha)-\mathcal{R}(\rho^t,\alpha)\|^2+\lambda_{un}\sum_{\alpha\notin\mathbb{A}}\|\mathcal{R}(\rho^t_r,\alpha)-\mathcal{R}(\rho^t,\alpha)\|^2 \] \( \mathcal{R}(\rho,\alpha) \)=可微渲染算子，\( \mathbb{A} \)=输入视角集；后两项分别约束输入视角和未知视角渲染图。原文：ScalarFlow 上 “we set \( \lambda_\rho \) to zero and use the reconstructed results from [7] as \( \rho \)”。输入<16 视图=coarse \( G^c_\rho \)，=16=fine \( G^f_\rho \)。
⑩ §3.3 机制④ — 渐进精化 NvRef（Eq.9-10）
动机（原文）：“in the absence of enough views, \( \rho^t_{r,c} \) still exhibits blurriness in novel views.” coarse 密度在新视角仍模糊。
精化（Eq.9）：\[ res^t_\alpha=\text{NvRef}\big(w^t_{r,\angle\alpha-\beta}\oplus w^t_{r,\angle\alpha+\beta}\oplus w^t_{r,\angle\alpha}\oplus\downarrow w^{t-1}_{f,\angle\alpha}\oplus\downarrow w^{t-2}_{f,\angle\alpha}\big),\quad w^t_{f,\angle\alpha}=res^t_\alpha+w^t_{r,\angle\alpha} \] 输入=相邻 \( \pm\beta \) 视图 + 当前视图 + 前两帧 refined 的降采样（\( \downarrow \)=2x 下采样），残差式精化。
损失（Eq.10）：\( \mathcal{L}_{NvRef}=\lambda_{mse}\|\cdot\|^2+\lambda_{l1}\|\cdot\|+\lambda_{res}\|Mean(res^t_\alpha)\|^2+\lambda_{sp}\|H(\cdot)-H(\cdot)\|^2+\lambda_{psnr}\|PSNR(\cdot)-PSNR(\cdot)\|^2 \)（L2+L1+残差+空间+PSNR 五项）。
渐进（原文）：“we set the maximum number of views to 16 ... categorize these 16 views into four types, namely clear, near, mid, and far views” 从近到远逐类 render→refine→重建下阶段密度（Alg.2），“gradually expands the set of reliable views”。
⑪ §supp 物理 — 速度估计 + advection + inflow
速度生成器 \( G_u \)（supp Eq.9）：\( \mathbf{u}^t_r=\mathcal{G}_u(\rho^t,\rho^{t+1}) \)，监督 \( L_u=\|\mathbf{u}_r-\mathbf{u}\|^2 \) + 无散 \( L_{div}=\|\nabla\cdot\mathbf{u}_r-\nabla\cdot\mathbf{u}\|^2 \)。
可微 advection（supp Eq.10-11）：\( \hat\rho^t=\mathcal{A}(\rho^{t-1},\mathbf{u}^{t-1}_r,\rho_{in},dt) \)，advection loss \( \mathcal{L}_{advect}=\lambda_{\hat\rho}\|\rho-\hat\rho\|^2+\lambda_\mathcal{R}\|\mathcal{R}(\rho)-\mathcal{R}(\hat\rho)\|^2 \)；再用 advected 密度回修速度 \( \mathbf{u}^t_r=\mathcal{G}_u(\hat\rho^t,\rho^{t+1}) \)。
inflow 逐帧估（supp Eq.13）：对每帧初始化随机 source \( \rho_{in} \)，最小化 advection 重建 L2 + 输入视角渲染 L2 + 时序平滑 \( \|\rho^{t-1}_{in}-\rho^t_{in}\|^2 \)，并 “enforce zeroing out portions of the source that exceed a height threshold” 防过估。最终 “effectively removing long-term flickers and non-physical artifacts”。
⑫ §4/§D 实验设置 · 数据集 · 指标
数据：ScalarFlow（real，“five cameras uniformly distributed along a 120◦ arc”，用前 20 个场景）；synthetic（Eulerian 法随机改风场/热场/inflow 区，“A total of 100 scenarios ... each scene containing 150 frames”）。
分辨率：real \( 64\times112\times64 \)，synthetic \( 64^3 \)，对比时各法密度场插值到 \( 64^3 \) 用 Houdini 渲染。
硬件：训练 “NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3090 (24GB)”，测试 “RTX 2080 Ti (11GB)”；DDIM 加速采样；两阶段训练（先 SvDiff/NvRef/\( G_\rho \)/\( G_u \)，后 fine-tune \( G_u \)）。
指标：MSE/RMSE/PSNR/SSIM/FID/LPIPS/STYLE（STYLE=VGG19 特征 Gram 矩阵的 L1 差），重建另用密度场 RMSE + 速度 divergence/gradient。

⑮ 泛化 / 下游应用 / 3DGS
泛化（原文）：对训练未见的 “smoke without inflow and horizontal plume”、“multi-plume collisions and dry ice” 均 work，“fundamentally different from the single-source smoke scenes in our training dataset”。
re-simulation：“the re-simulation result with added fluid-solid coupling”——50 帧时加球形障碍 / 外力场，把重建速度场投到新仿真域。
3DGS 兼容：“our method can be seamlessly integrated with ... 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) ... 3DGS is able to reproduce physically and visually plausible smoke sequences without the need for additional temporal processing.”
⑯ 亮点 / 创新点
① 用“显式合成侧视图 + 渐进精化”破单视角歧义，而非一次性多视角 diffusion（后者 shape-appearance 模糊）；② 把速度无散/平滑/inflow 等物理约束嵌进 diffusion 去噪与重建，保时序一致（去 flicker）；③ multi-frame 训练用“估出的 clean 图”当条件，专治 diffusion 长序列累积误差（F1→F4 实证）；④ coarse/fine 双密度生成器 + 16 视渐进，平衡效率与质量；⑤ 兼容 3DGS、支持 re-simulation / 加障碍物 / 外力等下游控制。
⑰ 疑问 / 边界 / 局限（原文 §G）
① “assumes a relatively clean background and consistent lighting conditions”——复杂背景/变光会退化；② “still relies on the accuracy of the initial side-view synthesis, significant errors in early stages can propagate”——强依赖初始侧视图，早期错误会传播；③ “primarily evaluated on synthetic and controlled real-world datasets”——户外多样烟雾未充分验证；④ “computational cost ... can still be significant when scaling to higher resolutions or longer sequences”；⑤ “focuses on grayscale smoke and does not explicitly handle colored smoke, solid obstacles, or interactions with complex environments”。[推测] NvRef 量化增益小（Table 6）说明其主要贡献在感知细节而非像素 MSE。
📌 全文一页总结（≥3 takeaway）
problem→method→experiment→takeaway：
problem：单视角动态烟雾重建 ill-posed，diffusion 新视角法多视角不一致 + 缺物理先验。
method：① 物理引导 SvDiff 逐帧造侧视图（multi-frame 训练防累积误差 + 速度无散/空间分布 guidance，Eq.1-6）；② 渐进 NvRef 从近到远 render+refine、coarse→fine 重建密度（UNet3+ 3D，Eq.7-10）；③ 可微 advection（NS）联合估速度+inflow（supp Eq.9-13）。
experiment：ScalarFlow/synthetic 上质量近 GlobTrans 但快约 120×（15min vs >30h），超 NGT/PINF/PICT/NeuSmoke，input 质量超 FluidNexus；消融证各物理约束 + multi-frame(F4) + 16 视有效。
takeaway 1：把 2D diffusion 合成与 3D 体一致性“循环交替 + 物理约束”，比一次性多视角 diffusion 更准更省。
takeaway 2：长序列 diffusion 的累积误差可用“估出的 clean 图当条件”的 multi-frame 训练显著缓解。
takeaway 3：物理 loss（无散 \( \nabla\cdot\mathbf{u}=0 \)）不仅提物理真实度，还直接改善时序一致性/去 flicker，且产物可 re-simulation。
边界：净背景/稳光照假设、依赖初始侧视图、仅灰度烟、长序列计算仍重。
