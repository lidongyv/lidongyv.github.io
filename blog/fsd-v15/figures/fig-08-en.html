BALANCE 1

Simulation fidelity

Looking good is not physically correct; collisions, occlusion and lighting can fool the policy.

BALANCE 2

Reward hacking

Policies find reward loopholes; real takeovers, comfort and rule constraints must anchor them.

BALANCE 3

Compute cost

Closed-loop rollout costs an order of magnitude more than supervised learning; folding it into pretraining is not free.
