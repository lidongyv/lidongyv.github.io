核心一句话：本文把「动作」改写成像素空间中被部分揭示的实体轨迹，使同一个预训练视频模型既能做正向动力学预测（揭示机器人运动、预测场景响应），又能做逆向行为合成（揭示目标物体运动、恢复机器人行为），仅用 15 小时数据微调即可跨本体泛化，并支撑策略评估、模型基规划与动作提取三种下游应用。

2 Abstract 全文逐字（L33–L51）

"Video models absorb rich priors over how the visual world moves, interacts, and responds to contact, making them promising substrates for robotic world modeling. The central challenge is how to communicate action to such models in a form aligned with the visual space in which they learned these interaction priors, yet still grounded in physical manipulation. We introduceMasked Visual Actions, a pixel-space control interface that expresses action as a partially revealed trajectory of an arbitrary entity in a video. Revealing robot motion makes the model act as a forward dynamics model that predicts the scene's response to low-level robot actions, while revealing desired object motion makes the same model recover robot behavior consistent with that outcome. Finetuned with only 15 hours of masked examples from real videos and simulation, a single checkpoint achieves strong visual fidelity and controllability across diverse scenes and multiple embodiments. In downstream manipulation settings, the model produces imagined rollouts whose outcomes correlate with real-world execution for policy evaluation, improves decision making by ranking candidate futures in model-based planning, and supports inverse modeling by synthesizing robot motion from desired object motion."

（"Preprint." 与 "arXiv:2607.19343v1..." 两行位于原文排版中摘要中间，已在第 1 节摘出。L38 的 "introduceMasked" 为提取丢空格伪影，照录。）

3 问题与动机

视频模型拥有远超机器人数据的交互先验（L58–L60）："Trained on large-scale observation, they accumulate remarkably broad priors over motion, contact, persistence, deformation, and change, far beyond what can usually be distilled from robot data alone. However, most still remain passive observers rather than tools for intervention."

核心问题是「动作如何以与视觉空间对齐的形式传达给视频模型」，原文问题句（L36–L38）："The central challenge is how to communicate action to such models in a form aligned with the visual space in which they learned these interaction priors, yet still grounded in physical manipulation." 现有控制信号的缺陷（L61–L63）："Existing models condition generation on text [ 53, 63], tracks [ 13, 20, 56, 58, 82], forces [ 21, 22, 44], keypoints [ 66, 70], or motor commands [19, 25], signals that are often sparse, embodiment-specific, or misaligned with the model's pre-trained visual experience."（方括号内空格为提取伪影。）缺失之物（L64–L65）："What remains missing is an action representation expressed directly in the visual space where pretrained video models learned their interaction priors."

正向/逆向同源的思想预演（L66–L68）："Once action is expressedvisually, the same model can complete different parts of an interaction depending on the trajectory revealed: revealing robot motion prompts a scene response, while revealing object motion prompts robot behavior." 动机起点还援引感觉运动控制理论（L53–L57）："In sensorimotor control, skilled behavior is often described as coupling a forward model that anticipates the sensory consequences of movement with an inverse model that recovers the movement needed to realize a desired state [67, 68, 69]. Robotic world models should similarly support both directions of reasoning in a single predictive framework."

动机链条可概括为：视频模型已学到交互先验，但现有控制信号（文本/轨迹/力/关键点/电机指令）要么稀疏、要么与本体绑定、要么与预训练视觉经验错位，因此需要一种「直接在视觉空间表达动作」的表征——这正是本文的切入点。

4 方法逐机制

4.1 像素空间控制接口与部分揭示轨迹。 接口定义（L38–L40）："We introduceMasked Visual Actions, a pixel-space control interface that expresses action as a partially revealed trajectory of an arbitrary entity in a video." 实现方式（L70–L72）："We finetune a pre-trained video model [63] to ingest actions as partially revealed spatiotemporal patterns in pixel space—a masked trajectory of an entity in the scene."

4.2 正向动力学 vs 逆向合成。 同一模型按被揭示实体切换角色（L72–L75）："When the revealed entity is the robot, the model predicts the scene's response and acts as a forward dynamics model; when the revealed entity is instead an object or desired object motion, the same model acts as an inverse model to recover robot behavior consistent with that outcome." 接口四性质（L76–L78）："While conceptually simple, this interface is pixel-aligned, embodiment-agnostic, native to video, and efficient to inject into a pretrained model through lightweight adaptation."

4.3 形式化（式 1–5）。 场景被建模为实体集合及其像素时空轨迹（L140–L146）："We view a scene S as a set of entities e1, e2, ..., en, and the video model generates a sequence of frames depicting how the entities interact over time. In the output video, each ei has a spatiotemporal trajectory, and we abuse notation slightly and write ei for both the entity and the spatiotemporal region of pixels it occupies."（式(1) p(V)=p(e1,...,en) 为实体轨迹联合分布，txt 中符号破碎。）条件化语义（L148–L155）："Conditioning on a subset S ⊆ {1, . . . , n}of entities yields the conditional distribution p({ei}i/∈S | {ej}j∈S , I0), (2) where I0 is a reference image of the initial scene. By varying S, the same model answers different questions about the same scene."（式(2) 在 txt 中版式破碎，此处按原文结构还原。）

掩码机制（L156–L166）："We realize this conditioning by masking. Let M∈ {0,1}T×H×W be a binary mask indicating which spatiotemporal pixels are revealed to the model (Mt,h,w = 1) versus predicted (Mt,h,w = 0)."；"and the model receives as input the masked video M⊙V together with the reference image I0."；"Training proceeds by sampling M from a distribution over masks and learning the conditional pθ(V|M⊙V, I0)."（式(3) M(S)=∪_{i∈S} ei，txt 中并集符号丢失。）

主动/被动二分（L168–L172）："We call entity ei activeif it acts on the scene through its own agency, such as a robot arm or a human, and passiveif its motion arises from interaction with an active entity, such as a manipulated object. Let A ⊆ {1, . . . , n}index the active entities and P={1, . . . , n} \ Athe passive ones."（"activeif"/"passiveif" 为丢空格伪影。）

正向模型（L173–L181）："Forward model.SettingS=A, we condition on the active entities and predict the passive ones, p({ei}i∈P | {ej}j∈A, I0).(4) This corresponds to the standard action-conditioned dynamics modeling, in which a robot's motion is provided, and the model simulates its effect on the scene. Unlike prior work that conditions on low-dimensional action commands [19, 25], the active conditioning here is supplied as masked videos that are agnostic to embodiments." 逆向模型（L182–L188）："Inverse model.SettingS=P, we condition on the passive entities and predict the active ones, p({ei}i∈A | {ej}j∈P , I0).(5) This direction has no analog in conventional action-conditioned world models: the user specifies a desired outcome in the world, and the video model recovers the agent behavior consistent with it."

4.4 零样本逆向。 训练只用主动实体掩码，逆向查询零样本泛化（L189–L195）："The active/passive distinction is a convenient way to describe how we use the model rather than a property of the model itself. The model is trained on masked video completion without any explicit notion of agency, and at inference, any subset S can be chosen. In fact, we trained our model only on masks depicting active robotic entities, yet it generalizes to queries conditioned on passive entities in azero-shotmanner. As observed in our empirical evaluations, this behavior is unique to our masked visual action conditioning, as conditioning the video model on sparser signals, such as low-level action commands or visualized skeletons, cannot achieve this level of generalization."

4.5 数据构建（双管道）。 数据来源（L198–L200）："We construct the masked modeling dataset by combining real-world videos from DROID [31] and simulation data from Robocasa [52]. We use both success and failure trajectories from both datasets." 分割管道（L202–L205）："Given any video, we can segment any entity in the scene using SegmentAnything [6], without the need for camera calibration or even explicitly knowing which robot is shown in the video. We use videos from DROID, and use the prompt “A robotic arm” for segmentation to isolate the robot." 其两大局限（L235–L239）："it suffers from two major limitations: First, it is challenging for the user to provide an exact segmentation mask of entities at test time. Second, any occluded regions in the robot would implicitly leak information about the scene dynamics from the original video." 渲染管道（L240–L242）："Instead of relying on segmentation, we can also align a robot mesh with the input video and use that as the masked conditioning. By rendering the robot mesh, we can visualize arbitrary action trajectories during inference and then use them as masked conditioning for the video model." 渲染细节（L247–L249）："To allow the model to see the full robot without self-occlusion, we render the robot only with translucent rendering and set the gripper fingers to bright red so the video model can easily observe the actions." 两管道互补（L250–L252）："Note that the rendering approach requires known camera calibration, and is limited to rendering the robot only as opposed to arbitrarily enabling masking any entity in the scene. As a result, we believe that the segmentation and rendering-based approaches are complementary."

4.6 模型实现与训练。 逐字（L269–L275）："We use Wan-Fun-Control 2.2 14B [63] as the base model. We encode the masked conditioning video using the same autoencoder as the video model and use concatenation as the conditioning mechanism. Concatenation is appropriate as the conditioning signal is spatially aligned with the desired output video. For the missing region from the masked conditioning, we set it to a uniform gray background. Instead of finetuning the entire model, we use LoRA finetuning with rank 256, and a batch size of 4 using 8 NVIDIA H200 GPUs. We train the model for approximately 10,000 steps over 4 days.F or reproducibility, we will release our code, data, and model weights."（"4 days.F or" 为排版伪影，应为 "4 days. For"。）

4.7 15 小时微调数据。 正文（L79–L83）："In addition to superior visual fidelity compared to prior work, we validate our framework across three applications in robot manipulation, both in simulation and in the real world: policy evaluation, model-based planning, and inverse modeling, where the video model is used as part of a robot policy. All experiments use asinglecheckpoint of the model finetuned on as few as 15 hours of robot interaction data." 图 1 注（L28–L29）："Efficiently finetuned on only 15 hours of data, a single checkpoint of the model can act as an action-conditioned forward model to simulate robotic interactions with diverse and unseen embodiments." 附录补充数据量（L875–L881）："We use approximately 1,000 DROID demonstrations with the two external cameras and process each demonstration with both the segmentation-based and rendering-based pipelines. ... We also include 4,000 examples from Robocasa across all tasks that do not involve robot navigation, and generate failure cases to incorporate in the training data."（另：测试集排除 CLVR、RAD 实验室场景，L863–L865。）

8 叙事关联【推断，≤5 条】

【推断 1】「轨迹作为通用接口」在运动学/力学二分法上的意义：论文把「机器人运动规定」（运动学侧）与「物体结果/接触响应」（力学侧）统一为同一像素时空轨迹的「部分揭示」，正向力学预测与逆向行为合成只是揭示不同实体，即运动学输入与力学结果在同一表征空间内互换角色。支持原句（L66–L68）："Once action is expressedvisually, the same model can complete different parts of an interaction depending on the trajectory revealed: revealing robot motion prompts a scene response, while revealing object motion prompts robot behavior." 及（L130–L131）："Our masking isspatial: active and passive entities live on the same pixel canvas, so the same forward/inverse switch also bridges the embodiment gap."

【推断 2】力学知识以「隐式交互先验」形式内嵌于视频模型，而非显式物理引擎：模型被要求的是补全被掩码场景的交互响应，接触/形变/响应由预训练先验承担。支持原句（L59–L60）："they accumulate remarkably broad priors over motion, contact, persistence, deformation, and change, far beyond what can usually be distilled from robot data alone"；及（L386–L387）："the model simply needs to model the interaction between the masked input and the rest of the scene."

【推断 3】跨本体泛化的证据与机制：泛化源于条件信号与输出同属像素空间，稀疏信号（骨架/末端位姿）则需显式学「稀疏动作→目标视频」对应关系，分布外即崩坏；掩码视觉动作在 BEHAVIOR 双臂本体上仍能「优雅泛化」。支持原句（L329–L330）："we demonstrate that Ctrl-world simply outputs static or corrupted videos for unseen embodiments, while our model can gracefully handle the unseen embodiment"；量化证据为表 1/表 2：BEHAVIOR 上 Ctrl-World LPIPS 0.196 vs Ours 0.123；消融中 End-effector vis. 0.171、Skeleton vis. 0.162、Ours 0.123。

【推断 4】「正向+逆向同一预测框架」直接呼应感觉运动控制的正/逆内部模型传统：引言以 Wolpert 等人 [67,68,69] 为出发点，论文的贡献正是把两类模型实现为同一条件分布的不同查询。支持原句（L56–L57）："Robotic world models should similarly support both directions of reasoning in a single predictive framework." 及贡献声明（L93–L94）："forward and inverse robot world-modeling problems can be cast as complementary conditional prediction problems of the same video model, obtained by revealing different entities in the scene."

【推断 5】该框架目前只保证现象学层面的轨迹一致性，不保证因果力学正确性：局限自陈「学相关而非因果」，故「轨迹作为通用接口」用于对力学因果有硬性要求的场景时受限，且能力上限被基座视频模型锁定。支持原句（L530–L534）："our model, similarly to existing generative models, learns thecorrelationbetween object interaction rather thancausal relationships, which remains an open research question. Furthermore, our method is naturally limited by the base video model's capabilities, in terms of both inference speed and what it can express, as it re-purposes the model's prior rather than modifying its capabilities."
