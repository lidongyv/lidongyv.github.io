2. Abstract 原文全文（逐字，txt L8–22）

"Abstract:Force signals provide critical interaction cues for contact-rich robotic manipulation. However, existing methods mostly use force as an additional observation modality, without fully exploiting its role in modeling future interaction dynamics or guiding execution-time feedback correction. In this paper, we proposeFA W AM, a force-aware world action model that incorporates force information at three levels: perception, prediction, and closed-loop execution. FAW AM first encodes historical 6-axis force/torque signals to modulate action generation, then jointly predicts future actions and end-effector wrenches to explicitly model contact evolution. It further introduces a residual correction module that uses the predicted wrench trajectory as an execution-time reference to refine actions online based on real-time force feedback. Real-world experiments across multiple contact-rich tasks show that FAW AM improves the average success rate by 36.25% over vision-only baselines and21.25% over existing force-aware baselines, demonstrating the effectiveness of our force-aware framework for robust contact-rich manipulation."

Keywords（逐字，txt L23–24）："Keywords:World Action Model, Force-Aware Policy, Contact-Rich Manipulation"

3. 问题与动机

论文论证链分四步：

(1) 视觉不足以判断接触状态：任务定义与必要性："Contact-rich manipulation tasks, such as wiping, peeling and pushing, are ubiquitous in human activities and real-world robotic applications. These tasks require the robot to continuously regulate its interaction with the environment." (txt L26–27)；"However, relying solely on visual observations are often insufficient, since critical interaction states, such as weak contact, excessive pressure or jamming, are difficult to infer from images." (txt L28–30)；因此 "force and tactile feedback provide essential complementary signals for capturing contact states that are ambiguous from vision alone [1, 2, 3, 4, 5]." (txt L30–31)。

(2) 现有力感知方法的两类不足：只当观察模态，或只做底层反应控制："these methods mainly use force as an additional observation modality for policy learning [4, 10], or introduce force feedback into low-level controllers for reactive control [11, 12]." (txt L34–35)；且不预测未来接触："they do not explicitly model the evolution of future contact states, such as contact forces. As a result, they lack predictive wrench references to guide execution-time corrections." (txt L37–38)。

(3) 人的类比——主动预测而非纯反应："humans performing contact-rich tasks continuously anticipate the contact outcomes of their motions and adjust when sensed feedback deviates from expectation." (txt L39–40)；"contact-rich manipulation is not purely a reactive observation-to-action mapping, but also a proactive prediction paradigm: the robot needs to anticipate how visual and contact states evolve under its planned action sequence." (txt L41–43)。

(4) 为什么必须是「世界动作模型 + 闭环」："World models [13, 14, 15, 16, 17], which capture future state evolution, provide a natural framework for contact prediction and contact-aware action generation. Yet, prediction alone does not ensure robust execution. Predicted contact signals should further serve as online references during execution, allowing the robot to detect contact deviations and correct its actions before failures occur." (txt L43–48)。即：世界模型负责「预见接触」，预测出的力轨迹在执行期充当「在线参考」闭环纠错。

4. 方法逐机制

4.1 总体架构（三层力集成 + 双频闭环）

三层次由摘要界定："a force-aware world action model that incorporates force information at three levels: perception, prediction, and closed-loop execution." (txt L12–13)。具体实现是两个不同频率的组件："As illustrated in Figure 2, FAW AM consists of a Force-Envisioned Action Model running at 1 Hz and a Force-Guided Residual Corrector running at 10 Hz." (txt L115–116)。分工是："The Force-Envisioned Action Model encodes recent force/torque histories into compact contact features and injects them into action generation through AdaLN-Zero-style modulation. It also jointly predicts future action and force trajectories, encouraging the model to learn the coupling between robot motions and their contact consequences." (txt L116–119)。

4.2 力感知如何嵌入 WAM

  • 接触特征编码："To provide the world action model with local contact-state awareness, we encode recent high-frequency end-effector wrench histories into a compact contact feature. Let w t ∈ R 6 denote the wrist force/torque measurement at time t, then the contact feature c t can be defined as c t = Encf (wt−Hf +1:t), (1) where Enc f is a lightweight MLP jointly trained with the action decoder." (txt L125–129)。
  • 传感器与滤波配置："An ATI Axia80-M8 6-axis force/torque sensor is mounted at the wrist to measure interaction wrenches." (txt L223–224)；"The ATI wrist force/torque sensor records 6-axis wrenches at 120 Hz. We apply a second-order Butterworth low-pass filter with a 10 Hz cutoff to suppress high-frequency sensor noise. For each policy query, the force encoder uses the most recent 0.5 s of wrench history, corresponding to 60 force/torque frames." (txt L527–530)。
  • 编码器规模："The force encoder is implemented as a three-layer MLP with a hidden width of 256 and is trained together with the force-aware action decoder." (txt L538–540)。
  • 注入方式（力条件 AdaLN-Zero 调制）："Building upon GE-Act [13], which decodes action trajectories from future visual latent representations, we inject c t into the action generation pathway through force-conditioned AdaLN-Zero modulation." (txt L130–132)；公式 (2)："We predict a force-conditioned offset and add it to the original AdaLN-Zero parameters: ¯u(i)t = u (i)t + MLP(i)f (ct). (2)" (txt L140–142)；零初始化保证平滑起步："The last layer of MLP (i) f is zero-initialized, so the model initially recovers the original action decoder and gradually learns force-adaptive refinements during training." (txt L144–145)。
  • 联合预测动作与力（不做孤立力头）："Instead of attaching an isolated force-prediction head, we replace the original action projection with a joint linear projection that outputs concatenated action and wrench trajectories. This encourages the decoder to learn a shared representation for action generation and contact prediction, capturing the coupling between robot motions and their physical contact consequences." (txt L146–150)。

4.3 世界模型与策略的耦合方式（两阶段训练）

  • 阶段一（微调视频世界模型）："In the first stage, we fine-tune the video generation model based on GE-Base [13] with a latent-space flow-matching objective." (txt L151–152)。
  • 阶段二（冻结世界模型、训练力感知动作解码器）："In the second stage, we freeze the video world model and train the force-aware action decoder." (txt L158)；把未来动作与力轨迹拼成 Z t = [A t , W t ]，解码器输出联合速度 [ˆv A t , ˆv W t ]（txt L160–162），损失为 "Laction = E [∥ˆvAt − (At − ϵ A)∥2²], L f orce = E [∥ˆvWt − (Wt − ϵ W )∥2²]. (4)" (txt L163–164)；总目标："The final action-force training objective is L stage2 = L action + λ F L f orce, where λ F balances the action generation and force prediction." (txt L165)；权重设定："We use λ F = 1 to balance the action and future-wrench prediction losses." (txt L538)。
  • 训练资源："Training is performed on 8 NVIDIA A100 GPUs." (txt L531)。视频世界模型微调 30,000 步、学习率 3×10⁻⁵；动作解码器 30,000 步、学习率 1.5×10⁻⁴（txt L535–537）。

4.4 训练数据（真实/仿真、力传感器）

  • 全部为真实世界实验："All real-world experiments are conducted on a Franka Research 3 (FR3), a 7-DoF robotic arm." (txt L222)；视觉："Visual observations are captured by three Intel RealSense D435i cameras, including one wrist-mounted camera and two static third-person cameras from the front and top views." (txt L224–226)。仿真数据：原文未写。
  • 演示与干预数据量："For each task, the base action model and all baseline policies are trained on the same 90 demonstrations. The residual corrector in FAW AM is trained with 20 additional human-intervention episodes that provide residual correction targets." (txt L235–237)；采集频率："Multi-view camera observations and proprioceptive states are recorded at 30 Hz, while 6-axis force/torque measurements are recorded at 120 Hz." (txt L237–239)。
  • 干预数据如何采集（FACTR 力反馈遥操作）："We collect correction data with FACTR [21], a force-feedback leader-follower teleoperation system. The follower arm remains under impedance control for safe and compliant contact, while the leader arm tracks the policy-generated command during autonomous execution rather than mirroring the follower feedback." (txt L201–204)；残差目标来源："When abnormal contact is observed, the operator corrects the remaining action chunk through the leader arm. Then, the difference between the corrected command and the base policy command is recorded as the residual target." (txt L205–207)。

4.5 残余校正器（执行期闭环）

  • 动机（开环动作块的脆弱性）："The action chunk generated by the action decoder is executed open-loop within its horizon, making it difficult to react to unexpected contact variations, such as insufficient pressure, excessive force or sudden resistance. Therefore, we introduce a lightweight force-guided residual corrector that runs at 10 Hz and adjusts the remaining actions within the current chunk." (txt L170–173)。
  • 共享冻结力编码器与力跟踪误差："we use the same force encoder as the world action model to encode recent wrench histories into the contact feature c t, with the encoder frozen during residual training. To expose contact deviations explicitly, we compute the wrench tracking error ewt−He+1:t = w meas t−He+1:t − w pred t−He+1:t , (5) where w meas denotes the measured 6D force/torque feedback, w pred denotes the predicted wrench trajectory and H e denotes the history window used to compute the wrench tracking error." (txt L176–182)。
  • 输入构成（式 (6)）：接触误差 e w t−He+1:t、接触特征 c t、即将执行的基动作块 a base t+1:t+Hp、预测未来力块 w pred t+1:t+Hp、本体感受状态 q t（txt L191–196）。
  • 门控（何时才修正）："we introduce a gating head that predicts a scalar residual gate g rest . At inference time, the gate is converted into a binary activation mask κ t ∈ {0, 1}, where κ t = 1 if g rest > τ and κ t = 0 otherwise. We manually set a conservative threshold τ = 0.8 to prevent the residual model from perturbing nominal executions." (txt L186–189)。
  • 修正公式与实现："a cort+1:t+Hr = a baset+1:t+Hr + κ t ∆a rest+1:t+Hr , (7) where a cort+1:t+Hr denotes the corrected action sequence and Res ϕ is implemented as a three-layer MLP for real-time inference." (txt L197–199)。
  • 训练目标与样本平衡："The residual corrector is trained with Lres = E [∥∆ares − ∆a gt∥2² + λ gate BCE (grest , yt)], (8) where ∆a gt is the ground-truth residual correction, y t ∈ {0, 1} indicates whether the sample comes from human intervention, and λ gate balances the residual regression and gate classification losses." (txt L213–216)；"Each training batch contains a balanced mix of intervention and non-intervention samples, encouraging the model to correct abnormal executions while preserving nominal base-policy behavior." (txt L210–212)；"We train it with a balanced batch of 128 samples containing 64 intervention and 64 non-intervention samples for 500 epochs." (txt L545–546)。

4.6 闭环评估怎么做

  • 标准评估协议："For each method, we perform 20 evaluation trials per task, with a maximum duration of 60 seconds for each trial." (txt L246–247)。
  • 扰动测试（执行期干扰）："Specifically, we manually introduce perturbations in four tasks: tilting the board during erasing, changing the cucumber height during peeling, moving the sand-filled box during pivoting, and changing the tail height of the vase during wiping. For each perturbation setting, we conduct five real-world trials." (txt L318–320, L336–338)。
  • 「闭环」的定义性表述："the correction model closes the loop by turning force discrepancies into corrective behavior." (txt L346–347)。

7. 局限 / 未来工作（原文逐字，txt L359–370）

"Despite the improved robustness, our method still has several limitations. First, due to the lack of large-scale video datasets with synchronized force/torque signals, our current framework does not integrate force into the video generation model, but instead couples actions and wrenches in the action branch. Second, the residual corrector is trained from a limited number of human intervention episodes, which may not cover diverse failure modes and limits recovery under large deviations. More diverse correction data under richer perturbations and failure scenarios may improve the correction performance. Finally, end-effector wrench prediction provides only indirect contact information. We plan to address this in future work by integrating explicit contact-state representations, such as local contact frames and dense tactile signals, to provide more direct and generalized action guidance."

8. 与叙事框架的关联【推断，共 5 条】

  1. 「闭环」在文中的定义与测量：闭环 = 以预测力轨迹为参考、由力偏差触发的 10 Hz 残余校正，非毫秒级在线。 - 支持原句："FAW AM consists of a Force-Envisioned Action Model running at 1 Hz and a Force-Guided Residual Corrector running at 10 Hz." (txt L115–116)；"By comparing the predicted force reference with real-time force feedback, it provides timely residual corrections and improves execution-time robustness." (txt L121–123)；"the correction model closes the loop by turning force discrepancies into corrective behavior." (txt L346–347)。 - 【推断】文中的闭环周期约 100 ms（10 Hz）；动作模型 1 Hz 为慢环，力传感 120 Hz 是快传感，但力信息经 0.5 s 历史窗口压缩成接触特征（txt L530）后才进入模型。原文未写毫秒级在线闭环；若叙事框架以毫秒级为闭环标准，本文只到 10 Hz，存在差距。
  2. 力反馈对「滑落/过度按压」类失败的作用证据：存在明确的定性证据句，但无按失败类型统计的数值。 - 支持原句："the full model benefits from both sensed force histories and predicted wrench references to avoid prolonged slipping or over-pressing."（Wipe Vase 任务分析，txt L589–590）；"making it difficult to react to unexpected contact variations, such as insufficient pressure, excessive force or sudden resistance" (txt L171–172)；"causing jamming in board erasing and toppling in box pivoting" (txt L341–342)。 - 【推断】「捏碎」原文未写，最接近的表述是 excessive force / over-pressing；证据形式是任务成功率（Table 1/3）加定性 rollout 描述，不是针对「滑落/捏碎」单独统计的成功率。
  3. 与「脊髓层」的对应与差距：残余校正器可类比快反射环（10 Hz、三层 MLP、冻结共享力编码器），但速度与输入滞后使其只能算「慢反射」。 - 支持原句："a cort+1:t+Hr denotes the corrected action sequence and Res ϕ is implemented as a three-layer MLP for real-time inference." (txt L197–199)；"we use the same force encoder as the world action model to encode recent wrench histories into the contact feature c t, with the encoder frozen during residual training." (txt L176–178)。 - 【推断】「1 Hz 慢认知（世界动作模型）→ 10 Hz 快反射（残余修正）」的双频结构可与皮层/脊髓分层类比；差距在于：10 Hz 远低于生物反射速度；接触特征来自 0.5 s 历史（txt L530），反射层输入本身滞后；门控 τ = 0.8（txt L188–189）使修正保守稀疏，并非持续在环。
  4. 「预测参考 + 偏差触发纠错」符合预期—反馈偏差控制叙事。 - 支持原句："Predicted contact signals should further serve as online references during execution, allowing the robot to detect contact deviations and correct its actions before failures occur."
