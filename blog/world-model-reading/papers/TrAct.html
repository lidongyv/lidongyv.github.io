2. Abstract 全文逐字（L14–36）

"Robot actions are inherently embodiment-specific and only weakly aligned with image-space visual changes, limiting their effectiveness as conditioning signals for robot world models. In contrast, visual tracks provide an embodiment-agnostic representation of how task-relevant points move through a scene, offering dense image-space guidance for accurate and spatially precise future video prediction. Building on this observation, we propose TrAct, a world-model-based robot decision-making framework that uses visual tracks as an intermediate interface between control and prediction. TrAct consists of three components: a Vision-Language-Action-and-Track model (VLAT) that jointly predicts candidate actions and corresponding visual tracks from the current observation and language instruction; a track-conditioned world model (TWM) that predicts future visual outcomes conditioned on the proposed tracks; and a vision-language reward model (VLAC) that scores the predicted outcomes. At inference time, VLAT generates candidate action–track pairs, TWM rolls out their visual consequences conditioned on the tracks, and VLAC selects the track whose predicted outcome best satisfies the instruction; the action paired with the selected track is then executed by the robot. Experiments on the proposed LIBERO-INTEGRAL benchmark and real-world Franka manipulation show that TrAct improves success from 27% to 55% in simulation and 49% to 76% on real-world tasks compared to the strong VLA baseline π 0.5. Furthermore, TWM consistently improves video prediction quality over AWM. These results suggest that visual tracks provide an effective shared interface between robot control and visual prediction, enabling more accurate world modeling and stronger robot generalization."

3. 问题与动机

核心论断：机器人动作与图像空间变化弱对齐，且动作是具身特定的（embodiment-specific），因此作为世界模型的条件信号不够好；视觉轨迹（visual tracks）是具身无关、稠密的图像空间引导，可作为替代接口。

引言（L42–46）：

"However, robot actions, which are typically used to connect the policy and the world model, are a weak interface between control and prediction: actions are embodiment-specific, and their visual effects depend strongly on scene geometry, object configuration, and contact dynamics. Thus, action-conditioned world models must infer dense visual futures from sparse, robot-specific commands."

动作到像素的映射欠定，导致动作跟随差、甚至幻觉成功（相关工作节，L84–85、L90–93）：

"Yet action conditioning remains poorly aligned with image-space change: actions are low-dimensional and embodiment-specific, while their visual effects depend on geometry, object state, and contact. This underdetermined action-to-pixel mapping causes poor action following; Liu et al. [15] find that such models often ignore erroneous actions and hallucinate success from visual priors."

轨迹作为替代（L47–50）：

"Visual tracks provide a natural alternative. They describe how task-relevant points move through image space, capturing object motion, contact regions, and interaction geometry, encouraging generalization. Because different robots can induce similar scene-point motion with different commands, tracks offer a compact, largely embodiment-agnostic representation for both policy learning and visual prediction."

摘要中同一动机的表述（L14–16）：

"Robot actions are inherently embodiment-specific and only weakly aligned with image-space visual changes, limiting their effectiveness as conditioning signals for robot world models."

4. 方法逐机制

4.1 VLAT（视觉-语言-动作-轨迹策略）

在 π0.5 上扩展（L126–140）：

"We build our Vision Language Action and Track model (VLAT) on top of the pre-trained flow-matching VLA policy π 0.5 [5]. We modify its action head to jointly output executable robot actions and 2D point tracks: πθ(ot, l)−→{(ai, τi)}Ki=1, (1) where ai ∈ RH×da is a predicted action chunk of horizon H and action dimension da, and τi ∈ RN×H×2 is a set of N predicted 2D point tracks over the same horizon. Following π 0.5, the PaliGemma backbone encodes the observations and instruction as a prefix, and the Gemma action expert denoises the output tokens via flow matching. We extend the action expert with a track stream: track and action tokens use separate projections but share the action-expert trunk. Track tokens precede action tokens, so action tokens attend to the track tokens but not vice versa. Tracks and actions are thus denoised jointly, yielding coupled action–track candidates."

联合 flow-matching 目标（L141–142）：

"The VLAT policy is optimized using a joint flow-matching objective: LVLAT = Lflow(a, a∗) + Lflow(τ, τ∗), (2)"

预测两类点、按视角不对称使用（L143–150）：

"We predict tracks for two types of points: (1) seven key points on the robotic gripper, and (2) a uniform 5×5 grid of scene points. We use these asymmetrically across camera views. In the agent view, VLAT predicts only the gripper points. Predicting environment dynamics is challenging, so rather than having the policy explicitly specify future scene motion, we offload this burden to the world model, which infers how the scene responds to the predicted end-effector motion. In the wrist view, the camera moves with the gripper, so VLAT additionally predicts the scene-grid points to capture camera motion relative to the environment. This yields 7 agent-view and 32 wrist-view tracks per timestep."

4.2 TWM（轨迹条件世界模型）

SVD + ControlNet（L151–161）：

"We adopt Stable Video Diffusion (SVD) [36] as the video generation backbone. To condition generation on the predicted tracks, we augment SVD with a ControlNet branch [37] that encodes track coordinates as spatial control maps. Specifically, the tracks are rasterized into blurred heatmaps, encoded by the SVD V AE, and injected into the UNet through a temporal ControlNet. Given observation ot and predicted tracks τ i, the world model generates a future video: ˆvi = pϕ(ot, τi) ∈ RH×C×Wimg×Himg, (3) where ˆvi is the predicted video rollout for candidate i. To support multi-view observations, we render view-specific tracks into separate ControlNet channels: agent-view tracks in red and wrist-view tracks in blue. This lets the world model distinguish view-specific motion while maintaining cross-view temporal consistency. This provides denser spatial guidance than raw action vectors."

AWM 基线（动作条件，L162–168）：

"For baseline comparison, we also train an action-conditioned variant that replaces track conditioning with action conditioning. Given a predicted action chunk ai, we first encode it using an MLP into the CLIP-L/14 feature space [38]. To support multi-view observations, we introduce a learnable camera-view embedding indicating agent-view or wrist-view actions. The view embedding is concatenated to the encoded action latent. The resulting view-aware action representation is then injected into SVD's UNet layers via cross-attention."

训练口径（L169–171）：

"Both world models are trained on ground-truth conditioning: TWM on ground-truth tracks (projected end-effector gripper tracks and CoTracker grid tracks) and AWM on ground-truth actions. At inference, both are conditioned on VLAT's predictions."

4.3 VLAC（奖励模型）

（L172–175）：

"To score predicted video results, we use the Vision-Language-Action-Critic (VLAC) model [33]. Given a predicted video and the instruction, VLAC judges task progress on a reward scale from 0 to 50. We adopt VLAC directly from the original authors, only finetuning it on our task data, and refer readers to [33] for implementation details."

4.4 预训练

（L178–187）：

"VLAT is pretrained on large-scale real-world robot datasets, including 76K DROID [39] trajectories and 150K EgoDex [40] trajectories (sampled from approximately half of the full EgoDex dataset) using a 1:2 dataset mixing ratio."

"Pretraining on diverse real-world manipulation data encourages the model to learn embodiment-agnostic visual motion representations that transfer across robot embodiments, camera viewpoints, and environments. All results in this paper use the DROID+EgoDex pretrained model trained for 30K steps with a batch size of 64 on 4 H100 GPUs."

（L188–190）：

"Both the track-conditioned (TWM) and action-conditioned (AWM) world models are pretrained on 76K DROID trajectories for 30K steps with a batch size of 64. During finetuning, both variants are trained on the same LIBERO and real-world datasets to ensure a fair comparison."

4.5 推理流程

（L194–205）：

"First, VLAT proposes K candidate action–track pairs {(ai, τi)}Ki=1. To make these candidates diverse, we apply Temperature-Scaled Resampling (TSR) during flow-matching sampling: we scale the initial noise magnitude to control diversity and resample from the learned policy distribution. Second, the track-conditioned world model rolls out a predicted video ˆvi for each of the K candidates, conditioned on its track τ i. Third, VLAC scores the K rollouts and selects the best: i∗ = arg max i∈{1,...,K} Rψ(ˆvi, l), (4) where Rψ denotes the reward model. The action of the selected pair is executed by the robot. We use K=20 candidates in simulation and K=16 in the real world, with a noise scale of 2 and TSR k=3 (simulation) and k=4 (real world)."

伪代码 Algorithm 1（L546–556）逐行要点（原文）："2: {(ai, τi)}Ki=1 ← PROPOSE(πθ, ot, l, K) ▷ sample candidate action–track pairs / 3: for i = 1 to K do / 4: ˆvi ← pϕ(ot, τi) ▷ roll out future video with predicted tracks / 5: si ← Rψ(ˆvi, l) ▷ score predicted outcome / 6: end for / 7: i⋆ ← arg maxi si / 8: Execute selected action chunk ai⋆ / 9: Observe new state ot+H / 10: t ← t + H / 11: end while"。

7. 局限与未来工作（原文逐字，L377–385）

"Limitations. Inference is the main practical cost. Each planning step produces a 16-step action chunk rather than a single low-level control command, and takes approximately 2.4 s on L40S GPUs; video-model denoising dominates (1810 ms), while candidate generation and reward scoring together take under 0.7 s (Table 5, Appendix). As in any propose–predict–select pipeline, compute grows with K; we mitigate latency by rolling out candidates in parallel, and the diffusion sampler, not the track interface, is the bottleneck, addressable through few-step samplers or distillation. The uniform 5×5 grid could be replaced by adaptive sampling of task-relevant regions. Extending to broader manipulation domains and longer-horizon tasks, and developing standardized benchmarks for comparing track-based approaches (e.g., ATM), are natural next steps."

8. 叙事关联（对快脑层「点云轨迹」的对应）【推断，共 5 条】

  1. 【推断】「轨迹作为控制与预测之间的中间接口」是 TrAct 的核心架构命题，与快脑层把「点云轨迹」作为控制模块与预测模块共享接口的设计同构——差异只在轨迹定义空间（2D 图像 vs 3D 点云），接口角色相同。支持原句："We propose 2D point tracks as an intermediate representation between robot policies and world models, improving both planning and future prediction."（L71–72）；"we propose TrAct, a world-model-based robot decision-making framework that uses visual tracks as an intermediate interface between control and prediction."（L19–21）
  2. 【推断】TrAct 用「embodiment-agnostic」论证轨迹优于动作，并用 UR5↔Franka 跨具身实验实证；快脑层以点云轨迹做跨车/跨场景泛化可沿用同一论证（轨迹比具身动作更本质）。支持原句："Because different robots can induce similar scene-point motion with different commands, tracks offer a compact, largely embodiment-agnostic representation for both policy learning and visual prediction."（L48–50）；"tracks serve as a consistent supervision signal across all datasets, whereas actions are supervised only when their control format is valid and executable."（L603–605）
  3. 【推断】TWM 把轨迹「栅格化为空间控制图」再注入生成模型，说明轨迹可作为空间先验条件化预测器；快脑层以点云轨迹输入预测网络时，也可采用类似的空间化编码而非纯序列向量。支持原句："the tracks are rasterized into blurred heatmaps, encoded by the SVD V AE, and injected into the UNet through a temporal ControlNet"（L153–155）
  4. 【推断】TrAct 强调轨迹接口使「预测的未来」变得可对任务指令打分（而非只在潜空间/几何空间评估），对应快脑层在轨迹空间评价候选预测/规划质量、用奖励选动作的设计。支持原句："TrAct instead uses tracks as the interface that renders a predicted future assessable against the task instruction."（L106–108）
  5. 【推断】真机背景替换（视觉域偏移）实验显示轨迹条件保持鲁棒（未见背景 0.70 vs 0.56），为「快脑层优先使用轨迹表征而非原始像素信号」提供旁证。支持原句："track conditioning anchors the world model to image-space motion that remains robust to background changes, consistent with Table 1 and Figure 3."（L365–367）
