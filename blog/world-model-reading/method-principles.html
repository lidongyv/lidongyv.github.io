P1 · 自回归 token 视频世界模型 (4 篇)

视频/文本/动作统一离散 token，逐 token 或掩码并行预测下一状态。

  • GAIA1
    arXiv:2309.17080

    生成式驾驶世界模型：把视频、文本、动作全部映射为离散 token、统一为下一 token 预测（自回归 Transformer），9B 级模型在 4.7 万小时驾驶视频上训练；涌现出高层结构、场景动力学与几何理解（原文自述的 emergent properties），可生成 4.4 分钟长的驾驶视频。

    A generative driving world model: video, text and actions are all mapped to discrete tokens and unified as next-token prediction (autoregressive Transformer) trained on 47k hours of driving video; emergent properties include high-level structure, scene dynamics and geometry understanding, with generations up to 4.4 minutes long.

  • WorldDreamer
    arXiv:2401.09985

    掩码 token 通用世界模型：VQGAN 16× 降采样（词表 8192）把视频离散化，用 STPT（U-ViT + 3D 卷积 + 时空窗口自注意力 + 空间交叉注意力）预测被掩码 token，推理按置信度逐步 unmask；相对扩散约 30 步/自回归约 200 步，本方法约 10 步、3×~20× 加速，24 帧 192×320 单卡 A800 仅 3 秒。

    A masked-token general world model: VQGAN at 16× downsampling (8192 vocab) discretizes video, and STPT (U-ViT + 3D conv + spatiotemporal windowed self-attention + spatial cross-attention) predicts masked tokens, unmasking by confidence at inference; versus ~30 diffusion steps or ~200 autoregressive steps it needs ~10 steps, a 3–20× speedup, generating 24 frames at 192×320 in 3 s on one A800.

  • GenAD
    arXiv:2402.11502

    生成式端到端驾驶：把场景表示为实例中心的结构化 token，用自回归 Transformer 生成未来场景演化（含自车轨迹），规划即生成；无需显式感知/预测/规划分阶段；nuScenes 上生成式规划指标优于分阶段基线。

    Generative end-to-end driving: the scene is represented as instance-centric structured tokens and an autoregressive Transformer generates the future scene evolution including the ego trajectory — planning is generation; no separate perception/prediction/planning stages; generative planning metrics beat stage-wise baselines on nuScenes.

  • Genie
    arXiv:2402.15391

    生成式可交互环境：时空视频 tokenizer + 自回归动力学模型 + 潜动作模型三件套，从 680 万游戏视频无动作标注训练；视频 token 经 ST-transformer 逐 token 预测、潜动作（|A|=8）作为加性嵌入；推理时丢弃 LAM、用户动作用潜动作编码；11B 参数、Robotics 2.5B 模型 FVD 82.7，200 个专家样本即可达 oracle 分数。

    Generative interactive environments: a spatiotemporal video tokenizer, an autoregressive dynamics model and a latent action model trained on 6.8M game videos without action labels; video tokens are predicted token-by-token by ST-transformer with latent actions (|A|=8) as additive embeddings, and the LAM is discarded at inference; 11B parameters, FVD 82.7 on the 2.5B robotics model, and 200 expert samples suffice to match oracle scores.

P2 · 扩散 / Flow-Matching 视频世界模型 (6 篇)

以去噪/流匹配生成未来帧，条件化方式决定可控性。

  • DriveDreamer2
    arXiv:2403.06845

    LLM 增强驾驶世界模型：LLM 把用户指令转成结构化交通元素（车/车道/相机），视频扩散按结构化条件+动作生成驾驶视频；AAAI 2025 发表；生成 FID 11.2/FVD 55.7，相对前作约 30%/50% 改进（结论段自述，需注意基线口径）。

    An LLM-enhanced driving world model: an LLM translates user commands into structured traffic elements (vehicles/lanes/cameras) and a video diffusion model generates driving video conditioned on these structures and actions; published at AAAI 2025; generation FID 11.2 / FVD 55.7, ~30%/50% improvement over prior work as stated in the conclusion (note the baseline scope).

  • DIAMOND
    arXiv:2405.12399

    扩散世界模型：在像素空间建模 p(x_{t+1}|x_{≤t},a_{≤t})，用 EDM 预条件 U-Net + frame stacking + AdaGN 条件化，推理仅 3 步 Euler 去噪即可稳定长时程；策略在扩散世界模型内用 REINFORCE+λ 回报训练；Atari 100k HNS 1.46（最强世界模型内训练），CS:GO 上 10Hz@RTX 3090 可玩。

    A diffusion world model: p(x_{t+1}|x_{≤t}, a_{≤t}) is modeled in pixel space with an EDM-preconditioned U-Net, frame stacking and AdaGN conditioning, stable over long horizons with only 3 Euler denoising steps; the policy is trained inside the diffusion world model with REINFORCE + lambda-returns; 1.46 HNS on Atari 100k and a playable 10 Hz CS:GO at RTX 3090.

  • Vista
    arXiv:2405.17398

    高保真驾驶世界模型：以 Stable Video Diffusion 为底座，用「首帧对齐 + 三帧 latent replacement」注入动态先验、双损失（动态增强 + 结构保持）训练；四种动作格式统一 Fourier 嵌入经零初始化 cross-attention 注入，训练时每样本只激活一种动作格式以保独立性；FID 6.9/FVD 89.4，并首次用模型自身不确定性构造无需真值动作的奖励。

    A high-fidelity driving world model on top of Stable Video Diffusion: first-frame alignment plus three-frame latent replacement injects dynamics priors, trained with dual losses (dynamic enhancement + structure preservation); four action formats share one Fourier embedding injected via zero-initialized cross-attention, with only one format active per training sample to keep formats independent; FID 6.9 / FVD 89.4 and the first reward from the model's own uncertainty that needs no ground-truth actions.

  • Cosmos
    arXiv:2501.03575

    物理 AI 世界基础模型平台：数据管线 2000 万小时→1 亿片段；tokenizer 提供连续 AE（4×8×8~8×16×16）与离散 FSQ（8,8,8,5,5,5=64,000 词表）两套；模型走扩散（DiT+EDM 7B/14B）与自回归（Llama3 风格 4B/12B）双路线并给后训练配方（相机控制、机器人 7 维动作、AD 六视）；权重开源，AR 4B 8×H100 达 10 FPS。

    A world foundation model platform for physical AI: a data pipeline from 20M hours to 100M clips; tokenizers offer both a continuous AE (4×8×8 to 8×16×16) and a discrete FSQ (8,8,8,5,5,5 = 64,000 vocab); models follow two routes — diffusion (DiT+EDM 7B/14B) and autoregressive (Llama3-style 4B/12B) — with post-training recipes for camera control, 7-D robot actions and six-camera AD; open weights, with AR 4B reaching 10 FPS on 8×H100.

  • GAIA2
    arXiv:2503.20523

    可控多视角驾驶世界模型：8.4B 时空分解 Transformer + flow matching，以自车速度/曲率（symlog）、智能体、环境、道路语义与外部潜嵌入为条件；200M tokenizer 384× 压缩、相机几何位置编码保多视角一致；训练语料约 2500 万条 2 秒视频（英/美/德），支持纯动作轨迹生成场景。

    A controllable multi-view driving world model: an 8.4B space-time factorized Transformer with flow matching, conditioned on ego speed/curvature (symlog), agents, environment, road semantics and external latent embeddings; a 200M tokenizer at 384× compression plus camera-geometry positional encodings keep views consistent; trained on ~25M two-second videos from the UK/US/Germany, capable of synthesizing scenes purely from ego action trajectories.

  • HumanoidWM
    arXiv:2506.01182

    人形机器人开源世界模型：以 1xGPT（100 小时 EVE 自我中心视频+25 维动作）为数据，比较 Masked Transformer（VQ-VAE+掩码交叉熵）与 Flow-Matching（Cosmos 连续 VAE 速度场+CFG）两种范式；参数共享（Full/Modality）近无损地降参 33-53%，单卡 A6000 可训、两卡可部署，Masked FID 10.13 优于 Flow。

    An open humanoid world model on 1x EVE data (100h egocentric video + 25-D actions) comparing Masked Transformer (VQ-VAE + masked cross-entropy) and Flow-Matching (Cosmos continuous VAE velocity + CFG) paradigms; parameter sharing (Full/Modality) cuts parameters by 33-53% with near-identical performance, trainable on one A6000 and deployable on two, with Masked-HWM at FID 10.13 outperforming Flow.

P3 · JEPA / 潜空间预测 (2 篇)

不重建像素，只预测未来潜表征（特征空间）。

  • VJEPA
    arXiv:2404.08471

    视频特征预测自监督：双编码器 + 窄预测器，在潜空间预测被掩码视频块的表征（ℓ1 + stop-grad + EMA 防坍缩），多块掩码（8 块×15% + 2 块×70%，平均约 90% 掩码率）；ViT-H/16 冻结评估 K400 81.9/SSv2 72.2，超最大视频模型至少 +5 点（SSv2），且样本少 1–2 个量级。

    Self-supervised video feature prediction: dual encoders plus a narrow predictor forecast representations of masked video blocks in latent space (ℓ1 + stop-gradient + EMA to prevent collapse), with multi-block masking (8 blocks ×15% + 2 blocks ×70%, ~90% masked); ViT-H/16 frozen gives 81.9 K400 and 72.2 SSv2, beating the largest video models by ≥5 points on SSv2 with 1–2 orders of magnitude less data.

  • LaLaWorld
    arXiv:2610.27851

    语言条件潜世界模型：把数值动作与视觉变化都用语言描述，让单一未来预测器在异构数据（仿真+机器人轨迹+图像/视频）上联合训练；预测器直接以预训练语言模型 Qwen3.5-0.8B 为骨干（读语言条件、预测未来 latent），冻结 DINOv2/DINOv3 编码器；规划性能与视频生成路线相当但训练成本 6× 更低、推理 13.9× 更快，LaLa-Policy 在 LIBERO/RoboCasa 与 Cosmos Policy 竞争且推理快 5.5×。

    A language-conditioned latent world model: numerical actions and visual changes are both described in language so a single future predictor trains jointly across heterogeneous data (simulation, robot trajectories, images/videos); the predictor itself is a pretrained language model (Qwen3.5-0.8B) that reads language conditions and predicts future latents, with frozen DINOv2/DINOv3 encoders; planning matches video-generation baselines at 6× less training cost and 13.9× faster inference, and LaLa-Policy competes with Cosmos Policy on LIBERO/RoboCasa at 5.5× faster inference.

P4 · 循环潜动力学与 MBRL (2 篇)

RSSM/离散 token 隐状态在想象中训练策略，样本效率优先。

  • IRIS
    arXiv:2209.00588

    离散潜世界模型：VQ-VAE 把帧编码为 512 码本 token，自回归 Transformer（20 层、256 维）逐 token 预测下一帧、奖励与终止；策略用 REINFORCE+λ 回报只在想象中训练；Atari 100k 人类归一化均分 1.046（+70% vs SPR），26 游戏 10 个超人类。

    A discrete latent world model: VQ-VAE encodes frames into 512-codebook tokens and an autoregressive Transformer (20 layers, 256-D) predicts next frame, reward and termination token by token; the policy is trained purely in imagination with REINFORCE + lambda-returns; 1.046 mean human-normalized score on Atari 100k (+70% vs SPR), superhuman on 10 of 26 games.

  • DreamerV3
    arXiv:2301.04104

    通用 MBRL 世界模型：RSSM 以离散潜变量建模动力学，配合 symlog 变换、free bits（1 nat≈1.44 bits）与 KL balancing 实现跨域鲁棒；策略在隐空间「想象」中训练、无 lookahead 规划，150+ 任务单一配置，Minecraft 从零采钻（1 GPU 9 天）。

    A general MBRL world model: RSSM models dynamics with discrete latents, stabilized by symlog transforms, free bits (1 nat ≈ 1.44 bits) and KL balancing for cross-domain robustness; the policy is trained in latent imagination without lookahead planning; a single config across 150+ tasks, including collecting diamonds in Minecraft from scratch on one GPU in 9 days.

P5 · 规划与模型预测控制（MPC） (3 篇)

用世界模型做前向 rollout，在动作空间里采样/优化（CEM/MPPI）。

  • TDMPC2
    arXiv:2310.16828

    隐式模型预测控制世界模型：不经像素重建，直接在潜空间学 encoder/dynamics/reward/value/policy 五件套，奖励与价值用 log 空间 101-bin 离散回归、潜表征用 SimNorm 归一化；规划用 MPPI 采样、以 terminal value 做自举，104 任务单组超参、317M 单智能体覆盖 80 任务。

    An implicit MPC world model: no pixel reconstruction — an encoder/dynamics/reward/value/policy quintet is learned directly in latent space, with reward and value as log-space 101-bin discrete regression and SimNorm latent normalization; planning samples with MPPI and bootstraps from a terminal value; one hyperparameter set across 104 tasks and a 317M single agent covering 80 tasks.

  • DINOWM
    arXiv:2411.04983

    预训练视觉特征上的世界模型：冻结 DINOv2 的 patch 特征（14×14×384）作为状态，约 19M 参数的 decoder-only ViT 做帧级因果预测；测试时以目标潜特征为代价、用 MPC+CEM 零样本规划；六仿真环境主表全胜（SR 0.98/0.96/0.92/0.90），但 CEM 规划 53s 为实时瓶颈。

    A world model on pretrained visual features: frozen DINOv2 patch features (14×14×384) form the state and a ~19M decoder-only ViT predicts frames causally; at test time an MPC+CEM planner optimizes a latent-cost to the goal for zero-shot planning; it wins on all six simulated environments (SR 0.98/0.96/0.92/0.90) but CEM planning takes 53 s, the real-time bottleneck.

  • SAFER
    arXiv:2611.20107

    世界模型规划的探索-利用解法：离线用「部分训练智能体 + 可恢复脚本扰动（hold/late-brake）」批量制造失败、由任意状态可查询的专家标注 (a,v)，22.06M 步语料；chunked latent WM 16 步一次前向、风险头输出有界 ρ̄∈[0,1]；规划用 MPPI（135 候选×2 迭代，J=return−W·ρ̄，W=50）——碰撞是「有界价格」而非开关；在线只精调 dynamics/scorers/risk 并用 EMA 慢副本打分；CARLA 路线完成 0.95。

    An exploration-exploitation solution for world-model planning: offline, partially trained agents plus recoverable scripted perturbations (hold/late-brake) stage failures, with an expert queryable at any state annotating (a,v) — 22.06M steps; a chunked latent world model runs 16 steps per forward, and a risk head yields bounded ρ̄∈[0,1]; planning uses MPPI (135 candidates × 2 iterations, J = return − W·ρ̄, W=50) so collisions are a bounded price, not a switch; online refinement touches only dynamics/scorers/risk with EMA slow copies; CARLA route completion 0.95.

P6 · 显式状态与可执行记忆 (4 篇)

把预测动力学/记忆做成显式可读状态，随动作演化并参与决策。

  • PhysicalRegistry
    arXiv:2610.16365

    显式动力学寄存器：在视频 DiT 内加 Tr=8 个 temporally grounded registry slots，每个去噪步由 observation-conditioned forecast 初始化、与视频 token 共享双向自注意力共演化，并经中间块 MLP 读头对齐 full-video JEPA 目标（λalign=0.5）；+0.45% 参数使 Cosmos Nano 16B 的 I2V 分数 43.56 超过 Super 64B 的 42.7；PRS 用中间 readout 偏离度选候选（+5.6–15.9%）。

    An explicit dynamics registry: Tr=8 temporally grounded registry slots inside a video DiT, re-initialized each denoising step from an observation-conditioned forecast, co-evolving with video tokens via shared bidirectional self-attention and aligned by a mid-block MLP readout to full-video JEPA targets (λalign=0.5); 0.45% extra parameters let Cosmos Nano 16B reach 43.56 I2V, above Super 64B's 42.7; PRS ranks candidates by intermediate readout departure (+5.6–15.9%).

  • EPM
    arXiv:2611.22294

    可执行知觉记忆：显式知觉状态 m̃ 与神经潜状态 ž 在想象中并步推进（Eq.2），read-out 进 reward/value/policy；奖励按「产生它的转移」对齐——用实测运动推进前一步记忆再配对奖励（Eq.4），防时序错位毒化训练；开环记录动作 rollout 前滚 15 步只训 reward head；Memory Maze 13×13 达 30.37 vs 23.37（+7.00，6/6 种子）。

    Executable perceptual memory: an explicit perceptual state m̃ advances alongside the neural latent state ž during imagination (Eq.2), with its read-out feeding reward/value/policy; rewards are aligned with the transition that produced them — the previous memory is advanced by measured motion before pairing (Eq.4), preventing timing-misalignment poisoning; an open-loop recorded-action rollout over 15 steps trains only the reward head; Memory Maze 13×13 reaches 30.37 vs 23.37 (+7.00, 6/6 seeds).

  • InertialWorld
    arXiv:2611.22314

    惯性视频世界模型：把「从已有状态演化」作为结构先验，Inertial Memory 双视图——稠密欧拉网格（场景对齐证据）+ 持久拉格朗日载体（局部内容绑定规范坐标）；每步固定顺序 carry→predict→update：按动作传输载体（速度保持 η=0.90）、从 carried 状态预测完整下一状态、学习门控（g0=0.020，默认持久化）合并；机器人/游戏/导航三域全胜基线（RT-1 PSNR 24.37）。

    An inertial video world model making 'evolve from what exists' a structural prior: Inertial Memory has two views — a dense Eulerian grid (scene-aligned evidence) and persistent Lagrangian carriers binding local content to canonical coordinates; each step follows carry→predict→update: carriers are transported by action-conditioned motion (velocity retention η=0.90), the complete next state is predicted from carried state, and a learned gate (g0=0.020, persistence by default) commits memory; it beats baselines across robot/game/navigation (RT-1 PSNR 24.37).

  • AgentWorld
    arXiv:2611.22880

    agent 中心 VLA 世界模型：固定 N=20 个离散 ⟨agent_i⟩ token，动态接地到周边参与者（语义身份/3D 几何/运动/交互），用几何门控+视觉门控路由锚定冻结 VGGT 4D 与 MLLM 2D 特征；训练三阶段（接地 SFT→推理 SFT + 去脚手架→GRPO 五项奖励）；Bench2Drive DS 81.17、延迟 0.31s，证明规划只需少量关键 token（去除图像线索仅轻微掉点）。

    An agent-centric VLA world model: a fixed set of N=20 discrete ⟨agent_i⟩ tokens dynamically grounds to surrounding participants (semantic identity/3D geometry/motion/interaction) via geometric and visual gated routing over frozen VGGT 4D and MLLM 2D features; three training stages (grounding SFT → reasoning SFT with scaffold annealing → GRPO with five rewards); Bench2Drive DS 81.17 at 0.31 s latency, showing planning needs few key tokens — removing image cues degrades only slightly.

P7 · 3D/4D 结构化与几何蒸馏 (7 篇)

occupancy/3DGS/对象运动学等结构化表示，或用视频先验蒸馏出几何动力学。

  • UniWorld
    arXiv:2308.07234

    世界模型预训练：用世界模型任务（未来几何/外观预测）在驾驶数据上做自监督预训练，再把表征迁移到下流 3D 感知/预测任务；无需人工标注，验证了「世界模型作为预训练范式」的价值。

    World-model pretraining: self-supervised world-model tasks (future geometry/appearance prediction) are trained on driving data and the representations transferred to downstream 3D perception/prediction; no manual labels, validating world modeling as a pretraining paradigm.

  • MUVO
    arXiv:2311.11762

    多模态 3D 世界模型：前视 RGB（600×960）+ 64 线激光 range view（≤6 万点）经 Transformer 自注意力融合，GRU 过渡模型在 2D 卷积潜空间演化，解码 RGB/点云/192×192×64@0.5m 二值 occupancy；自监督无标签，结论是 range-view 无损表示 + 2D 潜空间最优，BEV 映射反而变差（BEV 缺高度的瓶颈获反证）。

    A multimodal 3D world model: front-view RGB (600×960) and 64-beam LiDAR range view (≤60k points) are fused by Transformer self-attention, a GRU transition model evolves a 2D convolutional latent, decoding RGB, point cloud and 192×192×64@0.5 m binary occupancy; fully self-supervised, it finds lossless range-view representations with a 2D latent optimal while BEV mapping hurts — a counterexample to the BEV-height bottleneck.

  • OccWorld
    arXiv:2311.16038

    3D 占据世界模型：VQ-VAE 场景 tokenizer（码本 512×128 维）+ 空间聚合/时间因果注意力多尺度 GPT + U-net 聚合，两阶段训练，联合解码 occupancy 与自车位移；无需 instance/map 标注，4D 预测 mIoU 17.14/IoU 26.63，规划 L2 1.17、碰撞率 0.60。

    A 3D occupancy world model: a VQ-VAE scene tokenizer (512 codes × 128-D) plus a multi-scale GPT with spatial aggregation and temporal causal attention, trained in two stages and jointly decoding occupancy and ego displacement; without instance/map annotations it reaches mIoU 17.14 / IoU 26.63 for 4D prediction and L2 1.17 / collision 0.60 for planning.

  • CHORD
    arXiv:2601.04194

    4D 动态对象编排：把视频生成模型当高层「编排者」（Wan2.2 14B），用 RF-SDS 从 2D 视频的欧拉表示蒸馏出拉格朗日运动信息，以空间控制点 + Fenwick 树时间层级表示形变、流/ARAP 正则保物理合理；99 人研究对齐 87.71%、真实感 87.37%，蒸馏产物可驱动机器人操控。

    Choreographing 4D dynamic objects: a video generative model (Wan2.2 14B) acts as a high-level 'choreographer', and RF-SDS distills Lagrangian motion from the Eulerian representation of 2D video; deformations are represented by spatial control points with a Fenwick-tree temporal hierarchy, regularized by flow/ARAP; in a 99-person study alignment 87.71% and realism 87.37%, and the distilled motion can drive robot manipulation.

  • VerseCrafter
    arXiv:2601.05138

P11 · 评测与基准 (4 篇)

构造反事实/物理性质/多指标评测，审判世界模型的状态追踪能力。

  • WorldScore
    arXiv:2504.00983

    世界生成统一评测基准：把世界生成分解为「下一场景生成」序列任务（每步给相机轨迹+文字布局规范），用 3000 例测试集与三维指标（controllability/quality/dynamics，共 10 子项）统一评测 3D/4D 场景生成与视频生成；评测 20 个模型发现：视频模型相机可控性最高仅 40.22，静态 3D/4D 方法质量高但动力学弱。

    A unified benchmark for world generation: world generation is decomposed into a sequence of next-scene tasks, each specified by a camera trajectory and text layout, evaluated on a 3,000-example set with three dimensions (controllability/quality/dynamics, 10 sub-metrics) across 3D/4D scene and video models; on 20 models, video models' camera controllability tops out at 40.22 while static 3D/4D methods lead quality but lag dynamics.

  • ENACT
    arXiv:2511.20937

    具身认知评测基准：把具身认知评测写成「世界建模 VQA」——POMDP 中动作是场景图变化，两类序列重排任务：正向世界建模（给定动作重排打乱的观测）与逆向世界建模（给定观测重排动作）；8,972 个 QA（BEHAVIOR 仿真、长时程家庭活动）；前沿 VLM 与人类差距随交互视界扩大，且逆向任务普遍优于正向任务。

    An embodied-cognition benchmark that casts evaluation as world-modeling VQA: a POMDP whose actions are scene-graph changes, with two sequence-reordering tasks — forward world modeling (reorder shuffled observations given actions) and inverse world modeling (reorder actions given observations); 8,972 QA pairs over long-horizon home-scale BEHAVIOR activities; frontier VLMs trail humans increasingly with interaction horizon, and inverse tasks are consistently easier than forward ones.

  • Roz
    arXiv:2611.00612

    世界模型潜变量「品尝测试」：定义 10 个 contrastive 任务（三选一严格不等式）加 3 个 continuous 任务（最近邻排序），共 1,734 个场景，直接测潜变量是否编码质量/形变/摩擦/弹性；只需「模型吃帧/视频 + 提取潜 + 距离函数」单次前向。结论：图像稳健类任务全员高分，物理记忆类（occlusion/bounce/deform）全员崩溃；DreamDojo 重建 MSE 减半而 Roz 分数不变。

    A 'taste test' for world-model latents: 10 contrastive tasks (triple choice with strict inequalities) plus 3 continuous tasks (nearest-neighbour ordering), 1,734 scenarios in total, probing whether latents encode mass, deformation, friction and bounciness; it needs only frames/video, a latent extractor and a distance function, in a single pass. Results: all models do well on image-robustness tasks but collapse on physical-memory tasks (occlusion/bounce/deform); DreamDojo's reconstruction MSE halves while its Roz score stays identical.

  • FlipTest
    arXiv:2611.19822

    反事实世界状态测评：构造三级测试——视觉最小对（两帧几近相同但真实未来不同）、匹配控制（未来相同的位置不应翻转）、密集 sweep（精确定位翻转边界），共 1,680 初始帧（10 仿真 + 15 真实任务）；11 个视频世界模型最强仅 18.3% 对准确率（低于随机 25%），边界两侧预测 72%/89% 完全相同，告知结局后 18.3%→73.7%——模型学的是外观而非因果状态。

    A counterfactual world-state test: three levels — visual minimal pairs (near-identical frames whose true futures differ), matched controls (steps that leave the future unchanged), and dense sweeps (locating exactly where predictions flip) — over 1,680 initial frames from 10 simulated and 15 real tasks; all 11 video world models fall short, best at 18.3% pair accuracy (below 25% random), with identical predictions across 72%/89% of real/simulated pairs; told the outcome, accuracy jumps 18.3%→73.7% — they learn appearance, not the causal state.
